### 1. Design Advice (No Redesign)

1. **Responsive chrome trim:** Hide the unused `Compound` bar on the Lab tab, collapse the running slot to one compact strip on `phoneL`, and raise modal height on `ipadP`.
2. **Scroll & copy clarity:** Add a persistent list scrollbar/fade, auto-scroll the active monster into view, clarify `→ 1:` / `within 60`, and visually separate Sulfur from Shiny.
3. **Touch-safe controls:** Enforce ≥44 px targets on tabs, `?`, `Finish now`, and `Cancel`, push `Cancel` right as a secondary button, and apply standard raised button chrome in both themes.

**Pick:** **#1 first**—it unblocks the unusable `phoneL` viewport and reclaims vertical space on `d1280`/`ipadP` within the existing layout—then layer #2 and #3.
