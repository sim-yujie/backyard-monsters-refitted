You are the UX designer for Backyard Monsters Revamped (a browser remake of the old Flash game; desktop, iPad and phone; two looks: Modern = steel/wood painted HUD, Classic = Flash refreshed). Give DESIGN DIRECTIONS only (no code).

The map is a hex map. Each cell is a wild monster camp (tribe icon + level number), a player's main yard or outpost (building + name plate; a shield icon on the plate = damage protection), or water. Own cells have a cyan border.

NEW FEATURE (beta 9): LIVE map. When anything on a cell changes (owner changes / a camp or outpost is taken, a shield appears or ends, someone starts attacking it = "busy", level or damage changes), every player looking at that part of the map gets it pushed within 1-2 s over a live connection. Today the map silently re-asks every 60 s.
Scale target: 4,000-40,000 daily players. In a busy zone that means SEVERAL CHANGES PER SECOND on screen at once. The map now renders on demand: motion must be cheap (a short effect that stops; nothing running per frame when nothing changes; no permanent animation).

Three UX questions. For EACH, give 2-3 distinct directions (name them A/B/C), each with: what the player sees (exact look: shape, colour, size, duration, easing; Modern vs Classic differences), when it shows / does not show, how it scales to 10+ changes in a few seconds, phone vs desktop differences, and cost. Then say which one you recommend and why, in one or two sentences.

Q1 "Cell changed": how a cell shows it just changed. Options to consider: a short flash/pulse on the hex, an expanding ring, a brief glow on the plate only, or nothing at all (the cell just updates). Must stay calm when many cells change at once. Should own cells / cells in the player's range / the selected cell get anything different? Should different change kinds (taken, shield, busy) look different?

Q2 "Open info panel when its cell changes": the player has a cell's info panel open (picture d1280-modern-panel.png) and someone else starts attacking it, takes it (it becomes someone else's outpost), or a shield appears. What does the panel do? Options to consider: update in place with the changed line highlighted, a banner at the top of the panel ("Taken by X" / "Under attack" / "Shield up"), disable the Attack button with a reason, close the panel, keep the panel but ask before swapping content. The player might be about to press Attack at that very moment, so avoid buttons jumping under the finger.

Q3 "Connection lost": a small mark that the map is not live right now (the game then falls back to re-asking every 3-5 minutes). Where does it sit on desktop and phone (top bar, near the minimap, a notice chip at top centre like the existing "You are offline" notice), when does it show (only after a few seconds down, to avoid flicker on short drops), what it says (icons over words: short text, an icon), whether it can be tapped (retry), how it goes away (a brief "Live again" or silently).

House rules: short in-game text, icons over words, tap targets >= 44 px on touch, Modern and Classic both, nothing that flickers. Answer in plain text with headings Q1, Q2, Q3.
