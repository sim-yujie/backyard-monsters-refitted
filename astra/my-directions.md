# hcc-r3: my own directions for the Presets tab (written before asking astra)

Shared by all three: a two-part switch "Monsters | Presets" takes the row the round 2 mix tokens used (no new row).
The housing bar stays in the top strip (now/room, +queue, red when over). The "housing is full, N wait" warning
becomes a small red housing chip with the number at the right end of the tab row.

1. Rows with Send (list -> editor). Presets tab = N numbered rows (3 at L2, 5 at L3). Each row: number badge, the
   stacks as small portraits with counts, total goo, a Send button. Tap the row body to edit: the page turns to
   the monster grid + stepper, "Add" now adds to preset N; a Done button goes back. Send is one tap.
2. Slot rail + in-place editor. A row of N numbered slot tokens (like round 2); the chosen slot's stacks show as a
   rack under it, then the same monster grid + stepper (adds to the preset) and ONE Send button. Everything on one
   page, no second level; but it is tall on a phone and Send needs select-then-send (2 taps).
3. Count grid. Choose slot 1..N at the top; the monster grid itself is the editor: every monster card carries a
   count badge and -/+; Send at the bottom. Fast to build, but 18 cards with steppers do not fit a phone.

My pick: 1 (Send is the everyday action and should be one tap; editing is rare and can take a second step that
reuses the Monsters tab's own grid and stepper, so nothing new to learn).
