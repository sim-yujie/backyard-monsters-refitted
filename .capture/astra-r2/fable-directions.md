# Fable's directions, round 2 (written before astra was asked)

Owner, 21 Nov: HCC "not impressed by all" (A drawer, B Build|Queue switch, C yard rail).
Arrows: "the 'main outpost' text is very distracting and lengthy; cluttered; can mobile be better?"

## Outpost arrows (phone first; no yard name in the HUD)

- **F1 Three discs, no words.** Prev / flag / Next in one row above Map and Friends, as the owner's own
  reference, with every label removed. The flag disc says where you are with a picture and a number:
  a house on Main, a flag with the yard's place in the order ("3") on an outpost. The yard's name shows
  only for 2 seconds as it loads (a small toast), and in the list. Ends grey out.
- **F2 One capsule.** One 44 px high steel pill replaces the flag disc and its plaque:
  `< | house/flag 3/12 | >`. Reads as one control, is 30 px shorter than a disc row, the middle opens
  the list. Lowest clutter; smallest arrows.
- **F3 Page-turn tabs on the screen edges.** The dock keeps exactly today's buttons (flag with a count,
  no name). Two half-disc arrows sit on the left and right screen edges at thumb height, like turning
  a page of yards. Risk: they sit over the yard and near where a thumb pans.

## HCC L2/L3 (Hatch tab)

Rules for all: stacks are portrait + count only (no names, no "Empty" words); 10 stacks = 5 x 2 on a
phone, one row on desktop; the monster grid and the Add bar stay on the first phone screen.

- **H1 Numbered mix slots (loadouts).** Mixes are 5 numbered painted slots in one row beside the
  queue, like weapon loadouts: each shows the mix's top 3 portraits and its total, no names, no "Load"
  buttons. Tap a filled slot = load; tap an empty "+" slot = save the queue there; hold a slot =
  replace or clear. Outposts: one yard chip at the top of the tab (house = home); remote = the tab
  gets a coloured outpost band with a Home button.
- **H2 Same arrows, here too.** L3's yard choice is the outpost arrows again: `< house/flag 3/11 >` at
  the top of the Hatch tab steps through the yards that have an HCC; the middle opens the list. One
  idea learnt once, used in both places. Mixes are a strip of small "recipe cards" (portraits with
  counts + goo total) that scrolls sideways under the queue; the last card is "+" (save).
- **H3 Queue as a basket.** The window turns over: monsters on top, and the queue becomes a basket
  pinned at the bottom (10 slots, time, goo). A bookmark button on the basket opens the mixes. Tap a
  monster adds one, hold adds more: no stepper panel. Goes beyond the brief (changes how L1 players
  add monsters), so only as a contrast option.
