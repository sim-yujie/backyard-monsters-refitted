On a phone the panel is a bar fixed at the bottom.
Fixed decisions (owner, 21 Nov):
- L2: SAVED MONSTER MIXES (presets): save the current queue as a named mix, load a mix with one tap (it fills the queue with that mix, paying goo as usual). Number of mix slots not decided; assume 3-5. The queue grows from 7 to 10 stacks of 20.
- L3: stacks of 30 (still 10 stacks), plus "manage your outposts from home": from the main yard's HCC, pick one of your outposts THAT HAS ITS OWN HCC from a list (a player can own hundreds of outposts; yardtester has 11, 10 with an HCC), then edit THAT outpost's queue here as if standing in it (its own hatcheries, its own housing, its own goo? assume the outpost's own resources). Outpost mixes are saved separately from home mixes.
Design: where the mixes live (strip, dropdown, side tab...), how save/load/rename/overwrite works, how 10 stacks fit at 1280 (today 7 + totals already fill the width) and at 390, how the L3 outpost picker works and how the window makes it unmistakable that you are now editing OUTPOST X and not home (and how to get back), and how locked L2/L3 features show on an L1 HCC (upgrade teaser or hidden).
