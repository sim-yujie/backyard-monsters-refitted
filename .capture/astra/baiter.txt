
SCREEN 3: BAITER FOR FRIENDS
Today (pictures 1-3) the Wild Monster Baiter building opens "Baiter: test attack": make up any free army (monster cards with level picker, stepper, Fill; champion picker; Army size 0 / 1,500 = the cap from the Baiter's level) and test it against your own yard; a "Recent tests (N)" tab appears after a test: one line per test (e.g. "Your yard held · 42% damage · 3:12") with Watch and Report buttons; tests are forgotten on reload. The Friends window (pictures 4-5) lists friends; tapping a friend shows Visit / Message / Send gift.
Fixed decisions (owner, 21 Nov): You INVITE chosen friends or alliance mates to test YOUR MAIN yard. An invite lasts 24 h, with unlimited tests during it. The friend builds a free made-up test army like today's Baiter, capped by YOUR Baiter level. You then get a notice and can watch the replay and read the report, in their OWN list, separate from real defence replays (the game has a defence-replay list elsewhere).
Three screens to design: (a) SEND INVITE: where the button lives (Baiter window? friends list row? both?), picking several friends and alliance mates (a player can have dozens of friends + an alliance of up to ~50), seeing who is invited and time left, cancelling; (b) FRIEND'S SIDE: how the friend learns of the invite and starts a test (notice? a badge on Friends? a button on your yard when they visit you?), the army screen they see (reuse the Baiter army window with the inviter's cap), and what they see after; (c) OWNER'S SIDE: the notice that a friend tested you and the results list (who, when, result, damage, Watch, Report), with many tests from many friends.
