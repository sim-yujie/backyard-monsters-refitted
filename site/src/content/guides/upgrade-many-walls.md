## 1. Open the Yard Planner

On your yard, tap **Layout** (or press the **P** key). The planner's painted icon buttons are **Undo**, **Redo**, **Select**, **Box**, **Find**, **Upgrade walls** and **Re-arm**.

![The Layout button on the yard, next to Build](/images/guides/walls-layout.webp)

## 2. Pick your walls

- **Every wall:** tap **Find**, then **Walls**, then the wall row (for example *Block L1 × 400*).
- **Some walls:** tap **Box** and drag a box over them. **Shift-click** adds or removes one block.
- **On a phone:** tap the round **More** button, then **Selection**.

## 3. Upgrade walls

Tap **Upgrade walls**, then:

1. Pick a level, **2** to **5**.
2. Tap **Select all** or **Affordable**. **Clear** starts again. A button is greyed out when you can't afford it.
3. Check the cost, then tap **Upgrade**.

- Every wall is done **at once**. No builder, no waiting.
- Higher levels need a higher **Town Hall**.
- Walls already at that level are skipped, and cost nothing.

> The wall's own pop-up has no Upgrade button. Walls are always upgraded here.
