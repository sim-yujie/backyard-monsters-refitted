## 1. Press Upgrade walls

Two ways in:

- **Click any wall** on your yard, then **Upgrade walls** in its pop-up. That wall is already picked.
- Or tap **Layout** (or press the **P** key), then **Upgrade walls** in the planner's bottom bar. On a phone it is under **More**.

![The Layout button on the yard, next to Build](/images/guides/walls-layout.webp)

## 2. Pick a level, then your walls

Pick a level, **2** to **5**. The chosen level turns orange. Then pick your walls:

- **Drag a box** over walls. Only walls are picked, never towers or buildings. Each new box adds more.
- **Click or tap** a wall to add it, or to take it out again.
- **Select all** picks every wall. **Clear** starts over.
- **Affordable** keeps only as many walls as you can pay for.
- **Find:** in the planner, tap **Find**, then the wall row (for example *Block L2 × 369*).
- **On a phone:** turn **Box** off to scroll the yard, then back on to draw boxes.

A button is greyed out when it can't be used right now.

![The Yard Planner: Find, then the Block L2 row picks all 369 walls, shown marked on the yard](/images/guides/walls-select.webp)

## 3. Check the cost and tap Upgrade

The panel shows the cost. A red line means you can't afford them all: pick fewer, or tap **Affordable**. Then tap **Upgrade**.

<img class="narrow" src="/images/guides/walls-upgrade.webp" alt="The Upgrade walls panel: Level 3 chosen in orange, Select all, Affordable and Clear, 173 walls picked, the cost, then Upgrade 173" width="720" height="848" loading="lazy">

- Every wall is done **at once**. No builder, no waiting.
- Higher levels need a higher **Town Hall**.
- Walls already at that level are skipped, and cost nothing.
- **Cancel** or **Esc** stops picking.
