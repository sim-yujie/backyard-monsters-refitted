## 1. Open the Yard Planner

On your yard, press **Layout** (or the **P** key).

![The Layout button on the yard, next to Build](/images/guides/walls-layout.webp)

## 2. Pick your walls

- **Every wall:** press **Find**, then **Walls**, then the wall row (for example *Block L1 × 400*).
- **Some walls:** drag a box over them with the **Box** tool, or hold **Shift** and drag.
- **Shift-click** adds or removes one block.
- **On a phone:** tap one wall, then **Select all**.

![The Yard Planner: Find, then Walls, then the Block row picks all 400 walls, and Upgrade 400 walls appears](/images/guides/walls-select.webp)

## 3. Press Upgrade walls

Pick a level. Check the cost. Press **Confirm**.

<img class="narrow" src="/images/guides/walls-upgrade.webp" alt="The Upgrade walls window: how many walls, Raise to Level 2 to 5, and the cost" width="840" height="900" loading="lazy">

- Every wall is done **at once**. No builder, no waiting.
- Higher levels need a higher **Town Hall**.
- Walls already at that level are skipped, and cost nothing.

> The wall's own pop-up has no Upgrade button. Walls are always upgraded here.
