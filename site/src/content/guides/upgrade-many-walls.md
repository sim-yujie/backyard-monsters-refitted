## 1. Press Upgrade walls

Two ways in:

- **Click any wall** on your yard, then **Upgrade walls** in its pop-up. That wall is already picked.
- Or press **Layout** (or the **P** key), then **Upgrade walls** in the planner's bottom bar. On a phone it is under **More**.

![The Layout button on the yard, next to Build](/images/guides/walls-layout.webp)

## 2. Pick your walls

- **Drag a box** over walls. Only walls are picked, never towers or buildings. Each new box adds more.
- **Click or tap** a wall to add it, or to take it out again.
- **Select all** picks every wall. **Clear** starts over.
- **Affordable** keeps only as many walls as you can pay for.
- **On a phone:** turn **Box** off to scroll the yard, then back on to draw boxes.

![Picking walls: a box over one side of the yard, the picked walls outlined, and the Upgrade walls window beside it](/images/guides/walls-select.webp)

## 3. Check the cost and press Upgrade

Pick a level. The window shows the cost. A red line means you can't afford them all: pick fewer, or press **Affordable**. Then press **Upgrade**.

<img class="narrow" src="/images/guides/walls-upgrade.webp" alt="The Upgrade walls window: Select all, Clear, Affordable, Raise to Level 2 to 5, the cost, and Upgrade" width="840" height="900" loading="lazy">

- Every wall is done **at once**. No builder, no waiting.
- Higher levels need a higher **Town Hall**.
- Walls already at that level are skipped, and cost nothing.
- **Cancel** or **Esc** stops picking.
