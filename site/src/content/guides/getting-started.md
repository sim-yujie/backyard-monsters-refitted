## Make an account

Go to [play.bymrevamped.com](https://play.bymrevamped.com) and choose **Create account**. You need a username, an email address and a password. That's it: the game runs in your browser, so there is nothing to download.

> **Open beta:** the game is still being built. Things may break, and your progress may be reset before the full launch.

## The guided start with Bob

The first time you play, Bob, a friendly green monster, takes you through the basics. It takes about eight minutes. Bob points at each button with his hand, and the game pays for everything along the way. Here's what you'll do:

![Bob, the green guide monster, asking you to build a Sniper Tower while his hand points at the Build button](/images/shots/guided-start.webp)

1. **Collect** the twigs your Twig Snapper has made.
2. **Build a Sniper Tower**, then finish it for free.
3. **Watch a wild tribe attack.** Some Legionnaire scouts raid your yard, and your new tower sees them off.
4. **Build Monster Housing.** Bob gives you 15 free Pokeys to move in.
5. **Build a Map Room and a Flinger**, so you can find your enemies and fling monsters at them.
6. **Lead your first raid** on a practice Legionnaire camp that only you can see. If it goes wrong, Bob gives you a fresh squad to try again.
7. **Claim your Goals.** You finish several along the way, and they pay out big rewards.

You can skip the guided start, but you'll miss Bob's free army and the gifts.

## Your first week is protected

New yards are protected for **7 days**. Other players can't attack you while it lasts, so use the time to build up your defences and your army.

Be careful: **attacking another player ends your protection early.** Raiding wild tribes from your Map Room does not.

## What to do next

- **Open Goals.** The Goals button always shows what to do next, and every goal pays a reward.
- **Collect often.** Harvesters fill up and then stop, so empty them whenever you visit.
- **Keep your workers busy.** Every building or upgrade needs a free worker. Short jobs with 5 minutes or less to go finish for free.
- **Grow your Town Hall.** Upgrading it unlocks more buildings and lets you build more of each.
- **Tap the ? button** on any screen to see its tips again.
