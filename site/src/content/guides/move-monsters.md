Moving monsters now happens on **Map Room 2** itself. It replaces the old Move Monsters window.

## 1. Pick a yard

Tap **Map**, then tap one of your yards: your main yard or an outpost. On its card, tap:

- **Bring monsters here** to fill this yard from your other yards, or
- **Move monsters from here** to send this yard's monsters somewhere else.

## 2. Pick the other yards

Each of your yards shows a **label** with its biggest monster groups and its free room.

- **Bring here:** every other yard with monsters starts ticked. Tap a yard on the map to untick it. Your main yard gives first, then the nearest outposts.
- **Move from:** tap the yard that should get the monsters. Tap another yard to change it.

## 3. Pick the monsters

The tray at the bottom has one box per monster type. Tap a box, then **−**, **+** or **Max**. **All** fills the free room with every type in turn. Then tap **Bring … here** or **Move …**.

![Bring to Goo Spring: three yards send monsters, the target shows Full, and the tray has 19 monsters picked](/images/guides/move-map.webp)

## Good to know

- Moves are **free** and **instant**.
- A yard **under attack** can't send or receive monsters until the attack ends.
- **Smart moving:** attack a Map Room 2 wild camp yourself while you have a saved Repeat attack plan for it, and the game offers **Bring saved army?**. It moves the plan's monsters into Flinger range, then starts the attack. **Skip** moves nothing. Turn it off in **Settings ▸ Attacks ▸ Smart moving**.
