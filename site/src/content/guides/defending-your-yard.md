## Why defence matters

Wild monster tribes and other players can raid your yard and carry off your resources. Good defences make them pay for every step.

## Towers

Towers fire at any monster that comes into range. Find them on the **Defensive** tab of the Build menu.

- **Sniper Tower:** your first tower, and the one Bob helps you build.
- **Cannon Tower**, **Tesla Tower**, **Laser Tower**, **Railgun** and the **Aerial Defense Tower** unlock as your Town Hall grows.

Upgrade towers to make them stronger.

## Walls and traps

- **Blocks** are walls. Use them to slow attackers down and steer them past your towers.
- **Booby Traps** and **Heavy Traps** hide in the grass and go off when monsters walk over them.

Walls and traps don't need a worker: they finish as soon as you place them.

To raise a whole wall at once, see [Upgrade many walls at once](/guides/upgrade-many-walls/).

## The Monster Bunker

A **Monster Bunker** holds some of your monsters as defenders. Move monsters in from Housing, and they will help fight off attackers.

## Protection

When your yard is **protected**, other players can't attack it.

- New yards start with **7 days** of protection.
- You can buy more protection in the shop. It stacks on top of what you have.
- **Attacking another player ends your protection.** Raiding wild tribes in Map Room 1 does not.

## Test your defences with the Baiter

The **Wild Monster Baiter** lets you make up any army and send it at your own yard, so you can see how your defences hold. Nothing is lost, so try as many times as you like.

## After an attack

You'll get an attack report in your mail. Damaged buildings stop working until you repair them: tap a building to repair it, or use **Repair all** on the banner.

Tip: in a player's mail thread you can propose a **truce**. While it lasts, neither of you can attack the other.
