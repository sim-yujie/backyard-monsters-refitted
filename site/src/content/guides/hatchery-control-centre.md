The **Hatchery Control Centre** (HCC) runs all your Hatcheries from one queue. It now goes up to **level 3**.

## 1. Upgrade the HCC

Tap your HCC on the yard, then **Upgrade**. The cost is the same amount of Twigs, Pebbles and Putty, each.

| Level | Cost (each) | Time | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 4M | 25 hours | Town Hall 3, three level 2 Hatcheries |
| 2 | 25M | 2 days | Town Hall 6, four level 3 Hatcheries |
| 3 | 75M | 4 days | Town Hall 8 |

| Level | Queue | Presets | Outposts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 7 stacks of 20 | 2 | — |
| 2 | 10 stacks of 20 | 4 | — |
| 3 | 10 stacks of 30 | 6 | Runs them from home |

## 2. Save a preset

1. Tap **Monsters**, then the **Hatch** tab, then **Presets**.
2. Tap **+** on an empty slot.
3. Add monsters in the order you want them hatched. Tap **Done**.
4. Next time, tap **Send**. The preset goes to the end of the queue, as far as room and Goo allow.

![The Hatch tab at HCC level 3: a 10-stack queue and four saved presets, each with a Send button](/images/guides/hcc-presets.webp)

## 3. Run an outpost from home (level 3)

With your main yard's HCC at level 3, the Hatch tab can run your outposts' hatcheries without leaving home.

1. In the **Hatch** tab, tap the **arrows** at the top to step to an outpost. Its name shows in orange. Tap the middle to see all your yards.
2. Add monsters or **Send** a preset, as on your main yard.

![Running the outpost North Gate from home: its own queue, and a preset just sent](/images/guides/hcc-remote.webp)

## Good to know

- Only outposts with **their own HCC** can be run from home.
- An outpost's HCC goes to **level 2** (10 stacks of 20): 1M each of Twigs, Pebbles and Putty, 2 days, and two level 3 Hatcheries.
- All your outposts share **one preset list**.
- The Inferno's Incubator Control Centre stays at level 1, with no presets.
