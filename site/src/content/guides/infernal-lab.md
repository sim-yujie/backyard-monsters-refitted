The **Infernal Lab** is the Inferno's Monster Lab. It researches **one ability for each of the 8 Inferno monsters**.

> **Changed for monsters you already have:** without research, **Balthazar** does normal damage to monsters (it did 3x), and **King Wormzer** no longer splashes (it did 4x). Research brings both back. Moloch's raiders keep their 3x and 4x.

## 1. Build it

Build it in the **Inferno** from **Under Hall 5**, one per Inferno.

| Level | Cost | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 100,000 Bone and 100,000 Coal | Under Hall 5 |
| 2 | 300,000 each | Under Hall 6 |
| 3 | 600,000 each | Under Hall 6 |

## 2. Research an ability

1. Tap **Monsters**, then the **Lab** tab.
2. Pick a monster. The right side shows what the next rank does.
3. Tap **Research rank 1**. It is paid in **Sulfur**.

Ranks 1, 2 and 3 need the monster at level 2, 3 and 4, and ranks 2 and 3 need Lab level 2 and 3.

## The 8 abilities

| Monster | Ability | Rank 1/2/3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spurtz | **Fury:** faster and harder-hitting every second it fights | up to +5/+8/+20% |
| Zagnoid | **Regeneration:** heals part of its health every second | 1/1.5/2% |
| Malphus | **Dodge:** hits miss it (burns and poison still land) | 10/20/30% |
| Valgos | **Wall Crusher:** a blow on a wall also hits more walls nearby. Attacking only | 1/2/3 walls |
| Balthazar | **Monster Hunter:** more damage to monsters and champions. 2 ranks | 2x/3x |
| Grokus | **Shockwave:** part of each blow also hits nearby buildings. Attacking only | 30/45/60% |
| Sabnox | **Burn:** what it hits burns for 4 seconds, a share of the blow each second | 10/20/30% |
| King Wormzer | **Quake Splash:** its blows splash nearby buildings, wider each rank. Attacking only | 1x/2x/4x |

Ranks work when attacking and defending, unless the table says "Attacking only".
