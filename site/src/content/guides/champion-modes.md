## Pick a Mode before you attack

In the attack screen, every champion in your army list has a **Mode** box. Pick one. Your champion keeps it for the next attack.

![The army list in the attack screen: each champion has a Mode box, here Gorgo is set to Offensive](/images/guides/champion-modes-pick.webp)

## The three Modes

<ul class="cards">
  <li>
    <img class="icon" src="/images/guides/icon-swords.webp" alt="" width="56" height="56">
    <h3>Offensive</h3>
    <p>Goes for <strong>towers</strong> first. Finishes off hurt buildings. Ignores danger.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <img class="icon" src="/images/guides/icon-swords.webp" alt="" width="56" height="56"><img class="icon" src="/images/guides/icon-shield.webp" alt="" width="56" height="56">
    <h3>Hybrid</h3>
    <p>Hits the <strong>nearest</strong> target, like champions always have. Every champion starts here.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <img class="icon" src="/images/guides/icon-shield.webp" alt="" width="56" height="56">
    <h3>Defensive</h3>
    <p>Stays <strong>out of tower fire</strong>, sticks with its monsters, skips towers it can't beat.</p>
  </li>
</ul>

## Which one when?

| Yard | Try |
| --- | --- |
| Few towers, lots of loot | Hybrid |
| Towers shredding your monsters | Offensive |
| Champion keeps dying early | Defensive |

## Good to know

- <img class="icon icon--small" src="/images/guides/icon-health.webp" alt="Health"> <img class="icon icon--small" src="/images/guides/icon-damage.webp" alt="Damage"> <img class="icon icon--small" src="/images/guides/icon-speed.webp" alt="Speed"> A Mode never changes health, damage or speed. Only **what** it attacks.
- Your champion **learns** from its own attacks, and leans toward what worked in its Mode.
- Modes are for attacking. A champion defending your yard fights as always.
