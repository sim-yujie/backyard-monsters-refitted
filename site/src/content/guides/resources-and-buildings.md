## The four resources

Everything in your yard is paid for with four resources. Each one has its own harvester:

| Resource | Made by | Mostly used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Twigs** | Twig Snapper | Buildings and upgrades |
| **Pebbles** | Pebble Shiner | Buildings and upgrades |
| **Putty** | Putty Squisher | Unlocking new monsters, and some buildings |
| **Goo** | Goo Factory | Hatching monsters |

Harvesters work until they are full, then stop. Tap **Collect all** to bank everything at once, or tap one harvester to empty just that one. Upgrade a harvester to make it work faster.

## Storage

You can only hold so much of each resource. Build and upgrade **Storage Silos** to raise the limit. If a building costs more than you can store, the Build menu tells you that you need more silos.

## Shiny

**Shiny** is the game's special currency. You use it to skip waiting: **Finish now** speeds up a job that is already running, and **Instant** does a whole build or upgrade straight away. Early on these are cheap.

## Building and upgrading

- Tap **Build** to see every building you can add, sorted into tabs: **Resources**, **Buildings**, **Defensive** and **Decorations**.
- A greyed-out building tells you what it needs, usually a bigger Town Hall.
- Tap any building in your yard to **Upgrade** it. Upgrades make it stronger or faster.
- Every job needs a free **worker**. More workers mean more jobs at once.
- Jobs with **5 minutes or less** left can be finished for free.

## The Town Hall

Your Town Hall is the heart of your yard. Upgrading it unlocks new buildings and raises how many of each you can have. When you're not sure what to save up for, the Town Hall is usually a good bet.

## Repairs

Buildings damaged in an attack stop working until they are repaired. Tap a damaged building to repair it, or use **Repair all** on the banner.

## Plan your layout

The **Yard Planner** lets you rearrange your whole yard, and save layouts to come back to later.
