Everything on this page except the last section is new in **beta 5**.

## Modern or Classic

One switch: **Account settings ▸ Look and sound ▸ Style**.

- **Modern:** the new paintings, effects and music.
- **Classic:** the original Flash art, effects and music.

![The same monsters in Modern (painted) and Classic (original Flash pictures)](/images/guides/new-classic.webp)

## Painted windows

Every window has a painted frame. It follows the same switch.

- **Modern:** riveted steel and a yellow tin sign.
- **Classic:** cream parchment and a red ribbon, like the original.

![The same Champion Chamber window in Modern (steel) and Classic (parchment)](/images/guides/new-windows.webp)

## Quality, for slow internet

**Account settings ▸ Quality:** High, Medium or Low.

- **Low** loads smaller pictures and lighter music.
- On a slow connection, the game suggests Low once. It never switches by itself.

![Account settings: Style (Modern or Classic) and Quality (High, Medium or Low)](/images/guides/new-settings.webp)

## One tab per Hatchery

Two or more Hatcheries (and no Hatchery Control Centre)? Each one gets a tab with its level, its timer and its queue.

![The Hatch screen with one tab per Hatchery](/images/guides/new-hatchery-tabs.webp)

## Clear a queue in one tap

Added too many? **Clear all** empties the waiting queue and gives the goo back. It asks first. The monster hatching now keeps going.

![A Hatchery queue asking "Clear 43?" with the goo you get back, and Clear or Keep](/images/guides/new-clear-all.webp)

## Raise a champion at once

Pick a champion in the **Champion Cage** and press **Raise**. It is free, and your champion is in the cage straight away. No egg, no wait.

<img class="narrow" src="/images/guides/new-raise.webp" alt="The Champion Cage window with a Raise button under each champion" width="1000" height="950" loading="lazy">

## Champion Cage in one click

Click the cage: your champion is right there. Health, food, next level and **Freeze**.

<img class="narrow" src="/images/guides/new-cage.webp" alt="The Champion Cage window: the champion's picture, health, stats, feeding and Freeze" width="1000" height="1182" loading="lazy">

## Champion Chamber in one click

Click the chamber: every champion is a card. **Freeze** or **Thaw** in one tap.

![The Champion Chamber window: the caged champion and a frozen one, each on its own card](/images/guides/new-chamber.webp)

## Green for gains, red for losses

<p><strong style="color:#8ff0a8">+ green</strong> is what you got. <strong style="color:#ff9090">− red</strong> is what you spent or lost.</p>

<img class="small" src="/images/guides/new-signs.webp" alt="Shiny spent: a red minus amount under the Shiny counter. A reward: a green plus amount." width="615" height="140" loading="lazy">

## Also only in Revamped

- [Champion Modes](/guides/champion-modes/): choose what your champion attacks.
- [Upgrade many walls at once](/guides/upgrade-many-walls/) in the Yard Planner.
- Fog of war in the Map Room, and auto-attack for wild camps.
- No Flash, no download: it runs in any modern browser.
