## Your first monster: the Pokey

Every yard starts with the **Pokey** unlocked. Pokeys are slow and small, but what they lack in strength they make up for in numbers. Bob gives you 15 of them during the guided start.

## Unlock new monsters in the Monster Locker

The **Monster Locker** is where you unlock new kinds of monster. Each unlock takes putty and some time, and you can unlock one at a time. Stronger monsters need a higher-level Locker.

## Hatch them in the Hatchery

A **Hatchery** turns goo into monsters.

- Pick a monster and how many you want. You can queue several and come back later.
- Each monster costs goo, so keep your Goo Factories busy.
- More Hatcheries let you hatch more at once.

![The Hatch screen: five Hatcheries busy with Banditos, and painted portraits of every monster to pick from](/images/shots/hatchery.webp)

## Give them somewhere to live

Hatched monsters move into **Monster Housing**. When your Housing is full, hatching waits until there is room, so build and upgrade Housing as your army grows.

![The Housing tab: how much room is left, who lives in your pens, and the Monster Juicer](/images/shots/pens.webp)

Need space? The **Monster Juicer** turns monsters you no longer want back into goo.

## Make them stronger

- The **Monster Academy** trains a monster type to a higher level: more health and more damage.
- The **Monster Lab** researches a special ability for one monster type at a time.

## Champions

Build a **Champion Cage** to raise a champion: one big, powerful monster that fights alongside your army. Feed your champion every day to keep it growing.

## A few early monsters

Each monster has a favourite target. Mix them so every part of an enemy yard gets hit.

| Monster | Good at | Favourite target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pokey | Swarming in big numbers | Anything |
| Octo-ooze | Soaking up tower fire so others can get through | Defensive towers |
| Bolt | Running ahead of the pack | Harvesters, Town Halls and Silos |
| Eye-ra | Blowing up next to walls | Walls |
| Ichi | Shrugging off damage from towers and traps | Defensive towers |
