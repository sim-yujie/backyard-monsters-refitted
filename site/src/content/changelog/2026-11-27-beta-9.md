### Move monsters on the map

- **Moving monsters between your yards happens on Map Room 2 itself.** Tap one of your yards, then **Move monsters from here** or **Bring monsters here**. It replaces the Move Monsters window.
- Each yard shows a plate with its biggest monster groups and its free room. Moves are free and instant.
- **Smart moving:** attack a wild camp by hand with a saved Repeat attack plan, and the game offers **Bring saved army?**. Turn it off in Settings ▸ Attacks.
- How it works: [Move monsters between your yards](/guides/move-monsters/).

### Hatchery Control Centre levels 2 and 3

- **Level 2** (25M each of Twigs, Pebbles and Putty) and **level 3** (75M each): a longer queue, up to 10 stacks of 30.
- **Presets:** save a monster mix in the Hatch tab and send it to the queue with one tap. 2, 4 or 6 presets by level.
- **At level 3, your main yard's HCC runs your outposts' hatcheries from home.**
- Outpost HCCs go to level 2. In Modern, HCC levels 2 and 3 are new 3D buildings.
- How it works: [Hatchery Control Centre levels 2 and 3](/guides/hatchery-control-centre/).

### Infernal Lab

- **A new Inferno building** from Under Hall 5. It researches one ability for each of the 8 Inferno monsters, paid in Sulfur.
- **Without research, Balthazar does normal damage to monsters** (it did 3x), **and King Wormzer no longer splashes** (it did 4x). Research brings both back.
- How it works: [The Infernal Lab](/guides/infernal-lab/).

### Map Room 2

- **The map updates live.** A changed hex redraws with a ring. If someone takes the yard you have open, its Attack button reads **Taken · Update**.
- **Zoomed out,** alliance mates' and other players' yards merge into plaques with a count. Tap one to zoom in. Your outposts, your main yard and hostile players keep their own crests.
- **Repeat attack waits in line** when the server is busy, and starts by itself when your turn comes. Nothing is spent while you wait.
- Repeat attack checks your saved plan against the camp before it starts.

### Friends and alliances

- **Test your yard with friends:** your Wild Monster Baiter can invite friends and alliance mates to attack your main yard with a free army for 24 hours. Nothing is lost or won. Replays are in the Baiter's **Results** tab.
- **Last seen** on the friends list and the alliance Members tab: Online, Today, This week, 1-4 weeks or over a month.
- **Alliance goals:** Medium goals need 5 times as much and Hard goals 10 times as much. Easy goals and the points stay the same.

### Your yard

- **Yard arrows** step through your main yard, pinned outposts, then the rest. The middle opens your yard list.
- **Classic:** tiered towers show a I, II or III plate, matching Modern's plain, gold and black towers.
- **Feeding your champion:** fed monsters walk to the cage and burst into blood, as in Flash.
- **26 new decorations** for Glimmers, in both looks.
- Collecting on an Inferno yard throws Bone, Coal, Sulfur and Magma balls, as in Flash.

### Faster loading

- **Signing in is faster:** the first download is about 7 times smaller, and the rest loads while you type your password.
- Buildings load first; monsters, raids and music wait until your yard is drawn.
- On Low and Medium quality, Modern buildings use smaller pictures. Computers without a graphics card start on Low.

### Fixes

- Fewer black or missing towers on phones.
- Splash on buildings reaches the same buildings as in Flash, and an Eye-ra's blast reaches the defender it fights from the same distance as in Flash.
- Descent attacks with Krallen are no longer flagged by mistake.
- Before planned maintenance, a countdown shows on every screen.
- The Build window, the More menu, the Monsters tabs and the Lab buttons fit small and short screens.
