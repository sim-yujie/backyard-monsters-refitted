### Move monsters on the map

- **Move monsters between your yards on Map Room 2 itself.** Tap one of your yards, then **Move monsters from here** or **Bring monsters here**. Free and instant. It replaces the Move Monsters window.
- **Smart moving:** attack a wild camp yourself with a saved Repeat attack plan, and the game offers **Bring saved army?**. Turn it off in Settings ▸ Attacks.
- Guide: [Move monsters between your yards](/guides/move-monsters/).

### Hatchery Control Centre levels 2 and 3

- A longer queue (up to 10 stacks of 30) and **presets** you send to the queue with one tap.
- **At level 3, your main yard's HCC runs your outposts' hatcheries from home.**
- Guide: [Hatchery Control Centre levels 2 and 3](/guides/hatchery-control-centre/).

### Infernal Lab

- **A new Inferno building** from Under Hall 5: one ability for each of the 8 Inferno monsters, paid in Sulfur.
- **Without research, Balthazar does normal damage to monsters** (it did 3x), **and King Wormzer no longer splashes** (it did 4x). Research brings both back.
- Guide: [The Infernal Lab](/guides/infernal-lab/).

### Map Room 2

- **The map updates live.** If someone takes the yard you have open, its Attack button reads **Taken · Update**.
- **Zoomed out,** alliance mates' and other players' yards merge into plaques with a count. Tap one to zoom in. Your yards and hostile players keep their own crests.
- **Repeat attack waits in line** when the server is busy, and starts by itself when your turn comes. Nothing is spent while you wait.

### Friends and alliances

- **Test your yard with friends:** your Wild Monster Baiter can invite friends and alliance mates to attack your main yard with a free army for 24 hours. Nothing is lost or won.
- **Last seen** on the friends list and the alliance Members tab.
- **Alliance goals:** Medium goals need 5 times as much and Hard goals 10 times as much. Easy goals stay the same.

### Your yard

- **Yard arrows** step through your main yard and your outposts.
- **Classic:** tiered towers show a I, II or III plate.
- **Fed monsters** walk to your champion's cage and burst into blood, as in Flash.
- **26 new decorations** for Glimmers, in Classic and Modern.

### Faster and fixed

- **Signing in is faster:** the first download is about 7 times smaller, and buildings load first.
- Fewer black or missing towers on phones.
- Splash damage and Eye-ra's blast reach the same buildings and monsters as in Flash.
- Before planned maintenance, a countdown shows on every screen.
