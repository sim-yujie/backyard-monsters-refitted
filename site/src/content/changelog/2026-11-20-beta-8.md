---
title: "Beta 8: the Inferno in 3D, alliance trading and Repeat attack"
date: 2026-11-20
---

### The Inferno in Modern

- The Inferno's **Coal Extractor, Sulfur Swirler, Magma Pump, Strongbox, Incubator and Academy** are now 3D, and the **Siege Factory and Siege Works** on your main yard too.
- **All 8 Inferno monsters are repainted**, from Spurtz to King Wormzer.
- You can build a **General Store in the Inferno** (Under Hall 1).
- Classic is unchanged.

### Alliances

- **Trade tab:** swap one resource for another with an alliance mate. Each side gets 90% of what the other sends, up to 10% of your storage. One swap a day.
- **Goals** have their own tab, and the leader and officers can remove a goal.
- **Stars 5-10** of a constellation cost double. Stars already lit stay lit.

### Repeat attack

- If your yards in Flinger range are short of monsters, **Repeat attack moves them in from your other yards first**, and the button reads **Move and attack**.
- It costs **putty, paid when the attack starts**: 1M for camp levels 25-29, 1.5M for 30-34, 2M for 35-39 and 2.5M for 40-44.
- A plan is saved only from an attack on an untouched camp.

### Attacks and champions

- **Army, Catapult and siege weapon** are tabs in one row. **Auto-load last army** refills the Flinger after every fling.
- Each champion habit is **Active** or **Unproven**, and the Champion tab marks what your last attack taught it.
- **Krallen has no Mode** any more: she always fights as in Flash.

### Your yard

- **A built Yard Planner** unlocks layout slots 2-10, tower ranges, Find, Compare, Yard picture and picking many walls. Without one you get 1 layout slot.
- **Any wall can be upgraded on its own** from its pop-up, at once and with no worker. Blocks can be upgraded at any Town Hall level.
- **Upgrade preview** (Modern): the next level shows on your yard as a blue ghost.
- **Helping friends:** buildings show how often friends have helped, **Help all** speeds up everything you can in one tap, and arrows step to the previous or next friend.

### Empire and Thanksgiving

- **Outposts count** toward your empire score (a third of their buildings' cost), and the leaderboard shows how many outposts each player holds.
- **Golden turkeys** are now 1 in 10.

### Patches 8.1 to 8.7

- **Battles:** monsters no longer hit a building through a wall, ranged monsters attack from a little closer, and ranged shots land on the middle of a building. Monsters and buildings keep whole health, as in Flash.
- **Krallen** finishes off a harvester she has drained.
- **Attack results** no longer fail after the server has run for two days, and two players can no longer attack the same yard at the same moment.
- **Map Room 2** drags and zooms more smoothly.
