---
title: Open beta launch
date: 2026-10-10
---

Backyard Monsters Revamped is live! Anyone can now sign up and play at [play.bymrevamped.com](https://play.bymrevamped.com).

- **Free to play, right in your browser.** No Flash, no download, nothing to install. Sign up with a username, email and password.
- **A guided start with Bob.** New players can follow Bob, a friendly green monster, through their first buildings, their first defence and their first raid.
- **Someone to raid from day one.** Your Map Room always has neighbours to attack, even while the game is new and quiet.
- **New yards are protected for 7 days**, so you can build up before other players can attack you.

**Please note:** this is an open beta. The game is still being built, things may break, and **progress may be reset** before the full launch.
