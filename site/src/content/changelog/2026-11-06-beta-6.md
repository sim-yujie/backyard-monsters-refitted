---
title: "Beta 6: a new Map Room 2 and a yard in 3D"
date: 2026-11-06
---

### Map Room 2

- **A new look in Modern.** The map is one painted landscape with hills, cliffs, forests, rock fields, goo marsh, beaches and rivers, and you look across it at a tilt. Prefer it flat? Switch it off for your account.
- **Land matters.** Forest, rock and marsh cells change a cell's defence and income. Cells next to your own cells defend better, shown as a shield in the cell's pop-up.
- **Moving map.** On Quality High, water moves, foam runs along the shore, clouds drift overhead and gulls fly past. Medium keeps the moving water and foam. Low is still.
- **Tribe camp health bars.** A damaged camp shows a health bar over it, and heals 12 hours after the last attack.

### Your yard in 3D

- **Most buildings are now 3D in Modern,** with moving parts and their own damaged and destroyed looks. Classic keeps the original Flash buildings.
- **On the Inferno yard:** a new Incubator Control Centre on a lava pool, and new Inferno blocks.

### New

- **Quests.** A 7-day login card, 5 daily quests and 3 weekly quests, all paying Shiny. One free swap a day for a daily quest you don't like.
- **Monster Lab abilities.** Swarm for Pokey, Weakening Ooze for Octo-ooze, Thorns for Ichi and Critical Hits for Crabatron.
- **Decorations for Shiny.** The original game's decorations are on sale again, in the Build menu's Decorations tab.
- **The Juicer show.** Monsters walk into the Juicer, get blended, and goo and giblets spurt out.
- **Wild tribe camps rebuilt.** Every tribe camp has a new hand-made yard at every level, and the tribes are rebalanced.

### Sound

- **The original attack sounds are back:** tower shots, monster deaths, bombs, Wormzer and more.
- **Loot flies out of buildings** during an attack as orbs, with "+" numbers.
- **Separate volume sliders** for Music and Sound effects.

### Improvements

- **Font.** Settings ▸ Look and sound ▸ Font: Game font, Clear or Extra readable.
- **World chat on or off.** Settings ▸ Social ▸ World chat.
- **Tower range.** Hover over one of your own defences to see how far it reaches.
- **One "More" button** for Quests, Layout, Goals, alerts, Defences and Mail.
- **Yard Planner.** The bottom bar is now painted buttons: Undo, Redo, Select, Box, Find, Upgrade walls and Re-arm traps. Mirror, Align and Distribute are gone.
- **Upgrade walls.** No need to select walls first. Press Upgrade walls in the planner or in a wall's pop-up, pick a level, then pick walls yourself or use Select all or Affordable.
- **Account menu.** Your level now shows on your account button, and Settings has three tabs: Account, Look and sound, and Social.
- **Support the game.** A new item in the account menu with Patreon and Ko-fi links.
- **Shiny Lock.** The Shiny counter shows a padlock instead of 0 while Shiny Lock is on.

### Fixes

- Attacks keep running while the game's tab is hidden.
- Attacks on wild tribes, the Descent and Moloch count even if you leave or your connection drops.
- Attacks have enough time to save their result at the end, also under Declare War.
- Attacking a healed tribe camp straight from the map now fights the healed camp.
- The game loads in older browsers again.
- The Modern yard music plays again in Chrome, Edge and Firefox.
