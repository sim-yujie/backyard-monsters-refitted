title: "Beta 7: alliances, champion habits and Thanksgiving"
date: 2026-11-13
---

### Alliances

- **Alliances are back on Map Room 2.** Build an **Observatory** (4 million each of Twigs, Pebbles and Putty) to start or join one.
- **Three kinds:** Midas' Council (up to 15 players), Conquest Horde (30) and Fortress Legion (60). Each has four monster constellations, two it is strong in and two it is weak in.
- **Constellations** have 10 stars each, lit one at a time with points. Every star makes the whole alliance stronger.
- **Weekly goals** earn the points: 5 a week, Easy, Medium or Hard.
- **Territory:** every outpost a member holds earns a territory point each day, with monthly and all-time leaderboards.
- **On the map:** alliance chat, your alliance's shield under every member's yard, shared sight, and a warning before you attack a mate.

### Back from the original

- **Buy resources with Shiny** at the original prices: tap a resource in the top bar for +10%, +50% or Fill. **Top up** fills the gap when you are just short.
- **Map Room 1 plays by the old rules:** you attack the yards on your neighbour list, and anyone who attacks you joins it. No attacking players more than 7 levels below you; if one of them hits you, you get one revenge attack.
- **Slimeattikus learns Split** in the Monster Lab. **Rezghul raises the dead**: a defending Rezghul raises your own fallen defenders.
- **The original Attack window** is back on Map Room 2, with your monsters, champions and catapult bombs before you launch.
- **The Flash button clicks** and close sounds, and Flash's own mushrooms in Classic.

### Your champion learns how you play

- Each champion has three **Modes**: Offensive, Defensive and Hybrid. In each Mode it picks up **habits** from its battles, such as going for towers or guarding its monsters.
- A new **Champion tab** in the Monsters window. **Forget** wipes a Mode clean.

### Thanksgiving, 19 November to 3 December

- **14 days of treats**, turkeys to catch in your yard, and a **turkey ladder** with Shiny, a Scarecrow and the Autumn Maple Tree.
- **Glimmers**, a new event coin, buy decorations, including **six new autumn decorations**. All decorations now cost Glimmers instead of Shiny.

### Every weekend

- From Saturday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59 (UTC), a 30% boost, one kind at a time: **Builder's Weekend**, **Tribe Plunder**, **Study Weekend** and **Hatch Rush**. Check in on the Events card for 50 Glimmers a day.

### Your yard and friends

- **Walls in one go:** draw a whole line of walls and build it with one tap.
- **Yard ground** in Settings: Meadow, Autumn, Desert, Snow, Swamp or the original grass.
- **Player profiles** with a short bio and, from level 10, a photo. **Gift wishes**, **Gift all**, and a new **Friends window** with up to 10 pinned friends.
- **Frontier Pass:** for your first 10 days on Map Room 2, a cheap wild camp takeover and a free Starter Kit on 7 of those days.
- **Name and pin your outposts.** Map borders show friends, alliance mates and hostile players.
- **Krallen's loot aura** works at every level. Loot 3x your Krallen Hunt goal to keep it until the end of next month's hunt.
- **Modern look:** the Monster Locker and Bone Cruncher in 3D, a newly painted Under Hall, and every original decoration repainted.

### Patches 7.1 to 7.11

- **Helping friends:** no daily limit; your first 5 helps each day pay a reward.
- **Hatch tab** rebuilt to fit phones, with locked monsters behind a padlock.
- **Top bar** shows amounts in full up to 99,999. Tap a resource for the exact number.
- **Battles:** walls block exactly where they are drawn, and melee champions (Gorgo, Drull, Korath, Krallen) hit a building only from right beside it. Looted resources give points, as in Flash.
- **Phones:** iPhone and iPad no longer run out of memory on the yard; phones and tablets start on Medium quality.
- Many smaller fixes to raids, chat, the map bar on phones and quests.
