---
title: "Beta 3: friends, defence replays and tunnelling Wormzers"
date: 2026-10-19
---

### New

- **Friends.** Add friends and see them in a friends bar at the bottom of your yard. Visit a friend's yard, speed up their builds or repair their damaged buildings, and send them a gift. You'll find Add friend buttons in the Map Room, the mailbox and your alliance. Privacy settings let you hide your online status, turn off friend requests or block a player.
- **Defence replays.** On Map Room 2, open "Defences" in your yard to watch the last 20 attacks on your yard, at 1x, 2x or 4x speed, with a skip button.
- **Wormzers tunnel underground** like in the original game: they dig in, can't be seen or shot while they travel, and pop up to bite. Wormzers sent out from your bunkers tunnel too, so your own defenders sneak up on attackers.
- **New shots** for D.A.V.E. (twin rockets with smoke), Fomor (magenta orb), Zafreeti (sea-green healing orb) and Vorg, instead of the same orange fireball for everyone.
- Ranked-up Teratorns' fireballs hop on from building to building, like in the original game.
- Eye-ra now goes off in a proper fiery explosion with smoke and a scorch mark.
- Bigger hits feel bigger: Korath's quake shakes the screen, Wormzers burst out of the ground with a flash, and Bandito's whirlwind flashes.
- **Move to a random spot:** from the Map Room you can move your main yard to a random new spot on the map, free, once every 7 days. Your outposts are lost, and the game warns you first.
- Monsters now walk or fly off the edge of the yard at the end of an attack instead of just vanishing.

### Improvements

- Building with every worker busy: pick the spot first, then choose how to free a worker. The building goes up on that spot as soon as one is free.
- The yard view no longer jumps away after you place a building, and build timers count down steadily.
- Tribe raids now come at least 3 hours apart.
- "Skip tips" now closes all of Bob's tips at once.
- The account menu no longer sits on top of the zoom buttons.
- Clearer wording on the notice shown when your housing is too full.
- The friends bar now pages properly on wide screens when you have many friends.

### Fixes

- Eye-ra blasts now always damage the building they reach (before, about 2 in 5 blasts did nothing).
- Towers now aim at flying monsters where you actually see them.
- Some monsters now face the way they walk.
- Attacking a Map Room 2 yard that sits on a map edge no longer offers double the monsters and then refuses the result.
- A Wormzer's dirt mound no longer covers bunker roofs.
- Battles with lots of Banditos no longer stutter when they spin at the same time.
- Fixed a background server problem.
