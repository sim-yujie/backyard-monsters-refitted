---
title: Guided start for everyone, plus a way to support us
date: 2026-10-11
---

- **Bob's guided start is now switched on for every new account.** When you sign up, Bob walks you through collecting, building a Sniper Tower, housing your first Pokeys and leading your first raid. You can skip it, but you'll miss Bob's free army and gifts.
- **Support us on Patreon.** If you enjoy the game, you can now help pay for the server, website and email on [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/c/TheMonsterLab). The game stays free, and supporting gives no in-game advantage.
- **New credits page.** The sign-in screen now links to a credits page that thanks the Backyard Monsters Refitted team, whose server this game is built on, and lists the licences we use.
