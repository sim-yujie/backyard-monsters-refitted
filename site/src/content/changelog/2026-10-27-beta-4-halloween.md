---
title: "Beta 4: Halloween, events and champion eggs"
date: 2026-10-27
---

### Halloween

- **Your yard goes spooky** from 28 October, for two weeks: dark grass, and pumpkins grow where the mushrooms used to. The rare golden one is a glowing jack-o'-lantern.
- **Pumpkin hunt.** Tap a pumpkin to send a worker to pick it. Every pumpkin you pick counts towards prizes on the Events page: 10 pumpkins win 10 Shiny, 25 win 25,000 of each resource, 50 win 2 Mini-Pumpkins and 20 Shiny, and 100 win a D.A.V.E. Pumpkin and 30 Shiny.
- **Daily treats.** Log in each day of Halloween to claim a treat from the 14-day calendar.

### New

- **Events page.** The new Events button on your yard shows what's on now, what's coming next, the daily login calendar and the prizes you can claim. A badge shows when a treat is waiting.
- **What's new cards.** After an update, picture cards show you what changed. Open them again any time from the Account menu.
- **Krallen Hunt.** Every month from the 15th to the end of the month (the first is 15 to 30 November), take 100 million loot in attacks to win the champion Krallen, or raise yours one level, up to level 5. Raids on players, wild monster camps and tribes all count. For Town Hall 6 and up; you have a week after the hunt ends to claim your prize.
- **Free speed-ups.** Some treats are free speed-ups. They show up in the building panel as "Free (n left)" when you speed up a building.
- **Champions hatch from eggs.** Raising a champion now gives you an egg that hatches one day later. Nothing speeds an egg up. Changed your mind? Cancel the egg and pick again (the new egg takes a full day too).
- **Monsters on the move.** 15 monsters have brand-new walk and attack animations: Crabatron scuttles, Fang creeps, Eye-ra slithers, Bolt crawls, D.A.V.E. rolls on its treads, and lots more.
- **Bigger attack effects.** Monsters wind up and lunge into each blow, buildings flash and spark when hit and blow apart when they fall, damage numbers pop up bigger (and add together when lots of monsters hit one building), and the last blow plays in slow motion. Turn on "Reduce effects" in the Account menu for a calmer screen.
- **Report a bug.** Found something broken? Use "Report a bug" in the Account menu (or under the sign-in box) to tell us. A screenshot is added for you, and you can take it out or add your own picture.
- **Battle boosts in the Shop.** Tower Overdrive (600 Shiny) makes your towers hit 25% harder when your yard is attacked. Monster Overdrive (25% more damage), Monster Defensive Buff (30% less damage taken) and Monster Speed Buff (50% faster) cost 500 Shiny each and help your monsters in your attacks and when your yard is attacked. Each lasts 24 hours; buying one again starts its 24 hours afresh. An outpost can buy its own Tower Overdrive. Older defence replays are hidden once, because the game rules changed to add the boosts.
- **New achievement: Huge Rage.** Fire the Massive putty bomb from your Catapult in an attack to earn it and 10 Shiny.
- **Leaderboard.** "Leaderboard" in the Account menu ranks every player by empire points (your yard's points plus what its buildings are worth) and shows each player's Map Room 2 outposts. See the top 100, with your own place lit up, or "You: #N" if you're further down.
- **Wall upgrades in the Shop.** Turn every Block into stone, metal, gold or black diamond at once for Shiny (3 / 6 / 10 / 15 Shiny per Block for each step up). Needs Town Hall 3 / 4 / 5 / 6; outposts can buy them too. Blocks still being built or upgraded are finished, and damaged ones are healed.

### Improvements

- **Friends list upgrade.** Search for a friend by name, filter by "Can help", "Online" or "Gift not sent", and see at a glance who you can help or send a gift to. About twice as many friends fit on a phone screen.
- **Tidier on phones.** One Menu button now holds Map, Mail, Monsters, Alerts, Goals, Defences and the rest, and your friends tuck away behind their own Friends button.
- The battle screen on phones is clearer: the overview map and zoom fold behind one map button, and the top strip is a single thin row.
- On Map Room 2 on phones, Mail, My range and a new Filter button sit beside "Find a place".
- Map Room 1: online players show a green dot beside their name, and battles show as a shield (they attacked you) and a sword (you attacked them) with a count.
- Map Room 1 remembers how you sorted the list, and a new button flips it between highest and lowest first.
- **Special moves tuned.** Fang's venom bites once a second again. Bandito's whirlwind spins through enemy monsters (it never hurts buildings). When no building is near, Teratorn's fireball bounces on to a wall. Bolts defending from your bunkers can blink too.
- A new [Thank-you page](/support/thanks/) on this website for everyone who supports the game.

### Fixes

- Bob's tips no longer cover the What's new cards. They wait until you close the cards.
- Fixed a background server problem.
