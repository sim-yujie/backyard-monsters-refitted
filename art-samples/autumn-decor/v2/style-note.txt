STYLE NOTE: how the original Backyard Monsters (Flash, 2009) yard decorations look
(measured on the game's own decoration pictures, compared at full size)

1. Not painted cartoon art. They are small PRE-RENDERED 3D MODELS (late-2000s CG, like a 3ds Max
   render), shrunk to sprite size. Real-looking materials with soft grain: wood grain, bark,
   foliage made of many small leaf clumps, ribbed pumpkin skin, worn paint. No flat colour fills,
   no airbrushed "mobile game" gloss, no cel shading.
2. Camera: the same for every object. Seen from the front-left and from about 30-35 degrees above,
   so the top of round things and of bases shows a little; boxy objects are turned about 30-45
   degrees so two sides show (Outhouse, Lawn Chair, Birdhouse).
3. Outline: none. No black or coloured line around the object. The edge is just where the
   shaded object ends; the lower/back edges are dark because they are in shadow.
4. Light: one soft key light from the upper left / top front. Lit faces are only moderately bright;
   the right and lower sides fall into DEEP shadow (the darkest parts are near-black, about 15%
   brightness). Gentle ambient occlusion in creases, small soft specular highlights on smooth or
   wet surfaces only (pumpkin, acorn cap). Strong 3D form, not flat.
5. Palette: natural and somewhat muted / dusty, overall fairly DARK: the average brightness of a
   Flash decoration is about 35-55%, while the rejected samples averaged 60-90%. Greens are deep
   olive and forest green, wood is grey-brown or warm mid-brown. Only one accent colour per object is
   fully saturated (pumpkin orange, berry purple, flower red). No candy or neon colours, no glow.
6. No shadow and no ground painted under the object (the game adds its own drop shadow), no grass
   tufts, no base plate unless the object itself has one.
7. Detail and shape: simple, chunky, believable real-world objects, a little toy-like but NOT
   exaggerated cartoon proportions; no faces, eyes or cute expressions on natural objects. Few
   parts; every part must still read when the object is shrunk to 40-80 pixels tall.
8. Size in the game: 20-75 px tall at real size (Walnut 23, Acorn 30, Flowers 35, Beehive stump 46,
   Blackberry Bush 47, Bonsai Tree 61, Birdhouse 67, Scarecrow 72).
