STYLE NOTE: the MODERN look of Backyard Monsters Revamped (yard buildings, towers, traps)
(measured on the game's own Modern art in server/public/assets/buildings/*/top.3d1@2x.webp and
top.t1@2x.webp, compared at full size beside the Flash decorations; see refs/ref-modern-*.png)

1. Clean, modern, stylised 3D RENDERS (Blender), not paintings and not the gritty 2009 pre-render of
   the Flash art. Smooth, slightly bevelled shapes with crisp clean silhouettes; simple clean
   materials: light even wood grain on honey/tan planks with dark grain lines between boards,
   smooth painted or polished metal, neat stone blocks, glossy goo. Little surface noise.
   No cel shading, no painterly brush strokes, no flat colour fills.
2. Camera: the same as the buildings: seen from the front, about 35-40 degrees above, so tops show
   clearly; boxy things turned about 45 degrees so two side faces show almost equally (the Block
   crate, the Twig Snapper). Round things are seen as ellipses from above (Putty pot, Academy dome).
3. Outline: none. Edges are crisp and anti-aliased; small bevels on edges catch the light as thin
   bright lines, which is what makes the shapes read at small size.
4. Light: a warm key light from the upper left / front: tops and left faces bright, right faces in
   MEDIUM shade (darkest areas about 10-20% brightness, but most of a shaded face stays readable,
   not near-black like Flash); clear bright specular highlights on metal and glossy parts; soft
   ambient occlusion in creases and under overhangs.
5. Colour: clean and rich, a little brighter and more saturated than the Flash decorations
   (average brightness of a Modern building 30-55%, saturation 20-60%; the Flash-style autumn
   decorations are 21-35% bright). Honey and mid-brown wood, glossy golden orange, purple, signal red,
   steel grey with blue-white highlights, fresh slime/leaf greens. Accents strong but not neon, no glow.
6. Shape: chunky, a little toy-like, few big readable parts with clean forms (every part must read at
   40-80 px tall). Foliage and piles are built from fewer, bigger, smoother clumps/leaves with clear
   light and shade per clump (like the Goo Factory's goo), not thousands of tiny noisy leaves.
7. No ground, no grass, no base plate and no cast shadow painted under the object (the game adds its
   own shadow).

How Modern differs from Flash (Classic): Flash = soft, grainy, dusty late-2000s render, muted and dark,
near-black shadow sides, many tiny noisy details. Modern = crisp, clean, smooth modern 3D with bevel
highlights, richer and slightly brighter colour, readable medium shadows, specular sheen on hard parts.
