This page explains what information the game keeps about you, why, who else handles it, and how you can see or delete it. We try to keep as little as we can.

"We" means the volunteer team that runs Backyard Monsters Revamped. We are based in Singapore. You can reach us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

This policy is written to follow Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (the "PDPA"). It also gives players in the UK and EU the rights they have under their data protection laws (the "GDPR").

1. What we collect

When you sign up

  • Username. Other players can see it.
  • Email address. We use it to log you in and to send password-reset emails. Other players never see it.
  • Password. We never store your actual password. We store a scrambled version (a "bcrypt hash") that cannot be turned back into the password.
  • When you agreed to these terms and confirmed you are 16 or older. We record the date and time you ticked that box.

While you play

  • Your game progress. Your bases, buildings, monsters, resources, map positions, alliance, achievements and similar game data.
  • Battle records. Who attacked whom, when, and what was taken. These records include both players' usernames. They are deleted automatically after 12 months.
  • Account details. Your chosen avatar, map bookmarks, the list of players you have blocked, your total play time and number of sessions, and when you last opened your yard (we update this at most once an hour).
  • Messages:
    • World chat: each chat room keeps only its latest 100 lines. A room's lines are deleted automatically 30 days after the last message in it.
    • Alliance chat: each alliance keeps only its latest 50 messages. Older ones are deleted automatically.
    • In-game mail between players: deleted automatically 12 months after it was sent.
    • Chat reports: if someone reports a chat line, we keep a copy of that line, who said it and who reported it, so we can moderate. Deleted automatically after 12 months.
  • Notifications (such as "you were attacked"). We keep only your 20 newest, and none older than 7 days.
  • Fair-play checks. To catch bots and cheating, the game sometimes asks you a quick in-game question, and we log when this happens and the result. These logs are deleted automatically after 30 days. We also log game actions that look like cheating, such as a modified game client.

Technical information

  • IP address and browser type. Our game server records your IP address and browser details (your "user agent") with each request, when you log in, and with some game actions. We use this to keep the game running, stop abuse and investigate cheating. We keep these logs for up to 30 days, then they are deleted automatically.
  • Rate limits. To stop spam and password guessing, we briefly count requests from each account or IP address. These counts expire within minutes to hours.

What we do NOT collect

  • No real name, address, phone number or date of birth.
  • No payment details. We never see your card details (see "Supporters" below).
  • No advertising, no tracking pixels, and no analytics services selling or profiling your data.

4. How long we keep it

  • Your account and game progress: while your account exists. If you ask us to delete it, we delete it within 30 days.
  • Inactive accounts: we keep them until you ask us to delete them.
  • In-game mail, battle records and chat reports: 12 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Chat, notifications, fair-play logs: see the times in section 1.
  • Server logs with IP addresses: up to 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Backups: copies of the database are kept for up to 14 days, so deleted data can stay in a backup until that backup expires.

Beta resets. The game is in beta. If we reset game progress, we may keep your account (username, email, password) so you can still log in.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • See what data we hold about you, and how we have used or shared it in the past year.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Withdraw your consent, which means deleting your account (see below).
  • Delete your account and data.
  • Object to, or ask us to limit, how we use your data (UK and EU players).

Our data protection contact. Under the PDPA we have a person responsible for data protection (a "Data Protection Officer"). You can reach them at [CONTACT EMAIL].

There is no delete button or download button in the game. To do any of the above, email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the email address on your account and tell us your username. We will reply within 30 days, and we delete accounts within 30 days of the request. If we need longer, we will tell you why and when to expect an answer.

When we delete your account, we remove your login, email, game progress, your bases and land on the map, your alliance chat messages, your lines in world chat, and every in-game mail conversation you were part of (for both sides, since you can no longer reply). Some traces may stay for a short time or in a form that no longer identifies you:

  • Other players' battle records keep the fight, but your username is replaced with "Deleted player".
  • Chat reports about rule-breaking may be kept for up to 12 months to stop banned players from coming back.
  • Server logs and backups are removed when they expire (see section 4).

If you are unhappy with how we handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC). If you are in the UK or EU, you can complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

6. Browser storage and cookies

The game does not set any advertising or tracking cookies.

The game saves some small items in your browser's local storage:

  • Your login session. A login token (which includes your email address and player number), plus your username and avatar, so you stay logged in. The token stops working after 30 days, or when you log out.
  • Your preferences. Things like whether map ranges are shown, your map filter, base-planner overlays, whether you have seen a help tip, and the army you last used in an attack.

These are needed for the game to work, so we do not ask for permission first. You can clear them at any time by logging out or clearing your browser's site data.

Cloudflare's sign-up check may use its own small technical storage to tell humans from bots. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

7. Children

You must be 16 or older to play. We ask you to confirm this when you sign up.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you think someone under 16 has made an account, email [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will delete it.

8. Keeping your data safe

Passwords are stored as hashes, connections to the game use encryption (HTTPS), password-reset links expire after 20 minutes, and only the people who run the server can access the database. No system is perfect. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and, where the law requires it, the authorities.

9. Supporters

The game is free. There may be an optional supporter programme for people who want to help pay for the servers. It gives no gameplay advantage. If you join, the platform you pay through handles your payment details under its own privacy policy. We never see your card details. If we start one, we will add the platform to section 3.
