# refs: forest cliff
Use your image generation tool (one call) to create ONE new SQUARE image (1:1, as large as the tool allows) and save it as sheet.png in the current folder. Do not write code, do not draw with scripts.

The attached pictures are crops of a concept painting of a cartoon strategy game world map. MATCH ITS ART STYLE EXACTLY: bright, painted, toy-like cartoon, saturated yellow-greens and warm sandy yellows, crisp small painted details, warm sunlight from the top-left. Not photo-real. No text, no logos, no watermark, no hex lines, no grid lines, no borders, no buildings, no monsters, no number badges, no frames or labels around the pictures. Ignore the pale hex lines, buildings, monsters and badges in the attached crops: copy only the ground painting.

THE PICTURE: ONE single SEAMLESS TILEABLE TEXTURE filling the whole square: a dense FOREST CANOPY seen from above at the game's tilted camera, ONE SOLID MASS of rounded cartoon treetops packed tightly together with no ground showing between them, in MID and DEEP leafy greens exactly like the attached forest crop (mostly medium green crowns, deep green shadows between them, only the very tops of the sunlit crowns a lighter fresh green; NO bright yellow or lime crowns, no yellow cast), soft dark shading where crowns meet so the canopy bulges like the attached forest crop, with a few darker pine tips poking up here and there. Its left edge continues into its right edge and its top edge into its bottom edge, so copies laid side by side show no seam. Even detail all over (no vignette, no darker corners, no clearing in the middle). Each rounded crown is about one twelfth of the square wide.
