# refs: cliff hills rocks
Use your image generation tool (one call) to create ONE new image, landscape 3:2 (as large as the tool allows), and save it as sheet.png in the current folder. Do not write code, do not draw with scripts.

The attached pictures are crops of a concept painting of a cartoon strategy game world map. MATCH ITS ART STYLE EXACTLY: bright, painted, toy-like cartoon, saturated yellow-greens and warm sandy yellows, crisp small painted details, warm sunlight from the top-left. Not photo-real. No text, no logos, no watermark, no hex lines, no grid lines, no borders, no buildings, no monsters, no number badges, no frames or labels around the pictures. Ignore the pale hex lines, buildings, monsters and badges in the attached crops: copy only the ground painting.

THE PICTURE: pieces for painting rocky cliffs into the landscape, on a FLAT PURE MAGENTA background (#FF00FF) everywhere else, seen from a tilted top-down camera like the attached cliff crop.
- Top half: THREE long horizontal CLIFF FACE STRIPS, one above the other, each running the FULL WIDTH of the picture (edge to edge, so the strip can repeat sideways with no seam): a grey-brown rocky cliff face of vertical cracked rock columns, like the cliff in the attached crop, with a band of bright green grass overhanging its top edge and a few small stones at its foot. Strip 1 tall and steep, strip 2 lower and more broken, strip 3 mossier with small bushes. Leave a band of magenta between the strips.
- Bottom half, in a row: two SCREE PATCHES (a spread of grey stones and pebbles thinning out at the edges, fading into a few grass tufts), and three small ROCKY PEAK pieces (a pointed grey rock outcrop with a little snow on its tip; a jagged two-point outcrop; a low rounded rock knoll with grass on its sides), each with magenta round it.
