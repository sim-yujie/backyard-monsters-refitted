# refs: marsh
Use your image generation tool (one call) to create ONE new image, landscape 3:2 (as large as the tool allows), and save it as sheet.png in the current folder. Do not write code, do not draw with scripts.

The attached pictures are crops of a concept painting of a cartoon strategy game world map. MATCH ITS ART STYLE EXACTLY: bright, painted, toy-like cartoon, saturated yellow-greens and warm sandy yellows, crisp small painted details, warm sunlight from the top-left. Not photo-real. No text, no logos, no watermark, no hex lines, no grid lines, no borders, no buildings, no monsters, no number badges, no frames or labels around the pictures. Ignore the pale hex lines, buildings, monsters and badges in the attached crops: copy only the ground painting.

THE PICTURE: a sheet of 12 pieces in a 4 x 3 grid, each centred in its own cell with plenty of empty space round it, on a FLAT PURE MAGENTA background (#FF00FF) everywhere else, seen from the tilted top-down camera of the attached crop, each standing in a small ring of dark green marsh water at its foot (the ring fades out at its edge).
- Row 1: four TALL CATTAIL CLUMPS exactly like the attached crop's: dense bunches of tall thin yellow-green and green reed blades, some bending, with 4 to 7 brown cattail heads on thin stalks above them; each clump a little different (wide, narrow, leaning left, leaning right).
- Row 2: two more tall cattail clumps (one with a round green lily pad at its foot), a short reed tuft with two cattail heads, a shorter wide clump of grass-like marsh reeds.
- Row 3: a cluster of 4 round green LILY PADS floating flat on the water (one with a small pink water-lily flower), a cluster of 2 small lily pads, a FALLEN DEAD LOG lying diagonally half in the water (brown bark, broken end, a little moss), a grey ROCK half sunk in the water with moss.
