# refs: sea beach
Use your image generation tool (one call) to create ONE new SQUARE image (1:1, as large as the tool allows) and save it as sheet.png in the current folder. Do not write code, do not draw with scripts.

The attached pictures are crops of a concept painting of a cartoon strategy game world map. MATCH ITS ART STYLE EXACTLY: bright, painted, toy-like cartoon, saturated yellow-greens and warm sandy yellows, crisp small painted details, warm sunlight from the top-left. Not photo-real. No text, no logos, no watermark, no hex lines, no grid lines, no borders, no buildings, no monsters, no number badges, no frames or labels around the pictures. Ignore the pale hex lines, buildings, monsters and badges in the attached crops: copy only the ground painting.

THE PICTURE: a 2 x 2 grid of four equal square water textures for the game's painted sea.
Each square is a SEAMLESS TILEABLE TEXTURE seen from STRAIGHT ABOVE (top-down, only soft painted shading, no horizon, no perspective, no big single objects, no long cast shadows): its left edge continues into its right edge and its top edge into its bottom edge, so copies laid side by side show no seam. The detail is even all over the square (no vignette, no darker or lighter corners, no centre spot), small and dense, at the scale of the attached crops (a small flower is about 6-10 px in a square). The four squares touch edge to edge with NO gaps, NO gutters and NO frames between them.
- Top-left: TURQUOISE SHALLOWS: clear light turquoise water over pale sand, soft lighter ripple lines and small sparkles, like the shallow band next to the beach in the attached crops.
- Top-right: DEEP SEA: deep saturated blue water with soft darker and lighter swirls and small painted wave crests, like the open sea in the attached crop.
- Bottom-left: WET SAND: damp darker golden sand at the water's edge with tiny shells and a few thin water streaks.
- Bottom-right: SEA FOAM: white lacy surf foam in curving bands and bubbles over the deep blue water (the foam covers about half of the square, the blue shows between).
