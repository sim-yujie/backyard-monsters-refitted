# v2.0.0-beta.5.6

Country chat:
- Every country now has its own chat room. Pick your country in Account settings and your
  country's room appears as a tab next to Global.

Behind the scenes:
- When the game hits an error, the report now includes your account name and connection address,
  so we can find and fix problems faster. Only the game's admin sees them, and they are deleted
  after 30 days.
