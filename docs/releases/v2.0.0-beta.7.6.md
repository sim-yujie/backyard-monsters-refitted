# v2.0.0-beta.7.6

Changes:
- Helping friends: no daily limit any more. You can help as often as you like; the limit of 5 friends on one job stays.
- Helping friends: only your first 5 helps each day (UTC) pay a reward. After that the help panel says "Reward used up for today" and the help still goes through.
- Events: the next Krallen Hunt no longer shows as "coming up" while one is still running.

Fixes:
- iPad: Build, Goals, Events, Quests, Alerts and Defences no longer cut their list down to one row.
- Older iPads and iPhones (before iOS/iPadOS 16.4) now get the phone and tablet layouts.
- Short screens: Mail, Friends and building side panels keep their list on screen and scroll instead of running off the bottom.
- Phone: World chat steps aside while the More menu is open, so its buttons can be pressed.
- Map Room 2: an outpost whose Flinger finished building while you were away now shows the right range, so camps in range are no longer greyed out.
- Repeat attack: a camp out of your Flingers' range now says so in words instead of showing a server error.
- Wild raids: playing in two tabs at once no longer loses a raid's result.
