# v2.0.0-beta.5.3

## New look for the whole game
- Every window, bar and button now follows your Look and sound choice. Modern has steel frames
  with orange; Classic has Flash's brown frames with orange-yellow.
- This covers the yard buttons and bars, the Yard Planner, the maps, chat, the attack screen's
  tool bar and army sign, the loading screen, battle reports, the Baiter and the Inferno maps.

## Battles play more like Flash
- Brains and Bolts now break walls in their way instead of walking through them.
- Tower shells fly to where they land and blast there, as in Flash. A shell aimed at a monster
  that moves can miss.
- Bunkers follow Flash's rules for flying monsters and for the order they let defenders out.
- Defence boosts that run out during an attack now end during that attack.
- Monsters check their route the way Flash did, and don't turn onto a route blocked by a wall
  unless they are wall breakers.
- Balthazar goes for defending monsters and the defending champion before buildings, as in Flash.
- Malphus hops over walls.
- Rezghul no longer does double damage to towers. Rezghul, Teratorn and a ranked D.A.V.E. now
  loot at the full rate.

## Fixes
- A Tesla, Railgun or Laser that is destroyed now stops glowing, crackling and aiming.
- Kozu's two largest Map Room 1 yards now hold resources to loot.
- On phones, the Baiter's test window fits the screen and scrolls (a player reported it).
- The loading window's title is no longer cut off.
- When your protection runs out after ten minutes without playing, you are now disconnected, as
  with the idle disconnect. Before, moving the mouse kept you connected but open to attack.
