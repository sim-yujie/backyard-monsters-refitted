# v2.0.0-beta.6.11

Mail:
- New mail now pops up in the game as soon as it arrives, without reloading. Press the pop-up to read it.
- Moderators can now write to any player. Their mail carries a blue Mod badge, and blocking a player does not stop mail from moderators.
- Mail from the game team shows a green GM badge, as in chat.

Map Room 2:
- Wild monster camps in Map Room 2 give more loot, and more the harder the tribe and the higher the level.

Fixes:
- View yard from world chat and visits from the friends list open again, however far away the player lives on the map.
- Level 38 Kozu camps are back to the bigger yard they had in the original game.
- First Blood (Goals) can now be earned by defending against a real wild monster raid, not only in the starting guide.
