# v2.0.0-beta.7

Full details, with pictures, on Patreon: https://www.patreon.com/posts/175126446

## Alliances are back
- Team up with other players on Map Room 2. To start or join an alliance, build an
  **Observatory** in your yard (4 million each of Twigs, Pebbles and Putty, 36 hours to
  build). No level rule: on Map Room 2 with an Observatory, you're in.
- **Three kinds**, each with four monster constellations, two it is strong in and two it
  is weak in:
  - **Midas' Council**: up to 15 players and 2 officers. Strong in production (Wormzer)
    and loot (Brain).
  - **Conquest Horde**: up to 30 players and 4 officers. Strong in cheaper takeovers
    (Fang) and cheaper Starter Kits (Teratorn). All ten Teratorn stars lit: your Flinger
    reaches one cell further.
  - **Fortress Legion**: up to 60 players and 6 officers. Strong in building health and
    trap damage (Crabatron) and tower damage (Bandito).
- **Constellations.** Every constellation has 10 stars, lit one at a time. Each star makes
  the whole alliance stronger; stars in a weak constellation shrink that weakness. The
  leader and officers light stars; any member can suggest the next one. A full Midas
  constellation costs 600 points; bigger alliances pay more per star.
- **Weekly goals.** 5 goals a week, each Easy, Medium or Hard: destroy tribe yards, take
  over and hold outposts, win attacks and defences, loot resources, land catapult hits,
  upgrade buildings and more. Each kind has a goal of its own. Goals are the only way to
  earn points.
- **Territory.** Every outpost a member holds earns the alliance a territory point each
  day. Each kind has its own leaderboard, this month and all time.
- **On the map.** Alliance chat (it tells you when a mate is under attack and takes you
  there in one click), your alliance's shield under every member's yard (the leader picks
  it), shared sight on Map Room 2, and a warning before you attack a mate by mistake.
- **Find an alliance.** Browse by language, time zone and how they let people join, see
  who is online, or use **Quick Join**. Invites arrive in your mail.

## Back from the original
- **Buy resources with Shiny** at the original prices. Tap Twigs, Pebbles, Putty or Goo
  in the top bar for +10%, +50% or Fill, or find them in the Shop. **Top up** fills the
  gap when you're just short for an upgrade or a building.
- **Map Room 1 plays by the old rules.** You attack the yards on your neighbour list, and
  anyone who attacks you joins it. No attacking players more than 7 levels below you. If
  someone more than 7 levels below you hits you, you get one revenge attack, good for 14
  days.
- **Slimeattikus learns Split**, its Monster Lab ability: a ranked-up Slimeattikus leaves
  two or three smaller copies when it falls, on top of its usual Minis. Attack and
  defence.
- **Rezghul raises the dead**, as in the original. A defending Rezghul raises your own
  fallen defenders.
- **The original sounds**: the Flash click on every button and tab, and the close sound
  on every close button.
- **Flash's own mushrooms** are back in Classic, with newly painted ones in Modern.

## Your champion learns how you play
- The Champion Cage has three **Modes**: Offensive, Defensive and Hybrid. In each, your
  champion picks up **habits** from its battles (going for towers, loving loot, guarding
  its monsters, taking out Aerial Defenses first, staying close, playing careful when
  hurt). Korath learns to quake the crowd, and Fomor learns to back the big ones.
- A habit grows stronger the more it is used. **Forget** wipes a Mode clean.
- Your champion has its own **Champion tab** in the Monsters window. Click the cage,
  chamber, juicer or bunker to open it.

## Thanksgiving, 19 November to 3 December
- **A treat every day for 14 days**: resources, Shiny, speed-ups, a decoration on day 7,
  a Production Overdrive on day 12 (harvesters make double for 12 hours) and a Scarecrow
  plus 25 Shiny on day 14.
- **Catch the turkeys** that wander into your yard about once an hour. About one in four
  is a golden turkey with extra Shiny.
- **The turkey ladder**: 10 turkeys for 10 Shiny, 25 for 25,000 of every resource, 50 for
  a Scarecrow and 20 Shiny, 100 for the D.A.V.E. Pumpkin and 30 Shiny.
- Your yard dresses up for the season, and the Events page shows your calendar, ladder
  and Claim buttons.
- **Glimmers**, the new event coin: earned every day of Thanksgiving and at every step of
  the turkey ladder, up to 400 in all. Spend them on **six new autumn decorations**: Leaf
  Pile and Rake, Apple Barrel, Pie on a Tree Stump, Harvest Barrow, Turkey Topiary and the
  Autumn Maple Tree.
- All decorations are now bought with Glimmers instead of Shiny, at the same number.
  Decorations you already own stay in your yard.

## A boost every weekend
- Saturday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59 (UTC), every weekend brings a 30% boost, one kind at a
  time: **Builder's Weekend** (upgrades and Fortify cost less), **Tribe Plunder** (more
  loot from wild tribes), **Study Weekend** (faster Academy and Monster Lab) and **Hatch
  Rush** (faster hatching).
- A banner and a Friday reminder tell you what's coming. The first Builder's Weekend
  starts Saturday 14 November.

## Frontier Pass and Map Room 2
- **Frontier Pass**: for your first 10 days on Map Room 2, claim one set a day on any 7
  of those days: one wild camp takeover at 10% of the resource price and one free Starter
  Kit for any outpost. Already on Map Room 2? Your pass starts now.
- **The original attack window** is back on Map Room 2: your monsters, champions and
  weapons, catapult bombs included, before you launch.
- **Click your own yard or outpost** to see the monsters inside, its Starter Kit and its
  bonuses. The Starter Kit picker shows real pictures of each kit.
- **Name and pin your outposts**, and see all your yards in a tidy list.
- **Borders**: friends green, alliance mates dark blue, hostile players red, everyone else
  grey. The Key button explains them.
- A "Preparing map" counter shows how far along Map Room 2 is while it loads.

## Your yard
- **Walls in one go.** Draw a whole line of walls, corners and all. Double-click to
  finish, Backspace or right-click to undo, one total price, build it all with one tap.
- **Yard ground** in Settings: Meadow, Autumn, Desert, Snow, Swamp or the original grass.
  Classic gets Flash's own four grounds.
- **Yard picture.** One button in the Yard Planner takes a full-size picture of your whole
  yard with your name and level, traps hidden. Save it, or copy and paste it anywhere.
- **Quicker hatching.** The count starts at 0, hold the minus button to take monsters off,
  and no more "are you sure?" when you cancel.

## Friends and profiles
- **Player profiles**: a short bio and, from level 10, a photo. Shows Town Hall level,
  empire points, friends and alliance. Open one from a name in chat or a face in your
  friends list.
- **Hidden achievements**, and a painted badge for every achievement.
- **Gift wishes**: rank up to three resources you'd most like; friends see them when they
  send. Collect 10 gifts a day, and delete old gifts one at a time or all at once.
- **A new Friends window** from the round button beside Map. Pin up to 10 friends, add a
  friend from world chat, and see which world each friend is playing in.
- **Notifications** in Settings, one switch for each kind.

## Modern look, or Classic
- The Monster Locker and Bone Cruncher are rebuilt in 3D.
- A newly painted Under Hall.
- Every original decoration repainted for Modern, from the Eiffel Tower and Big Ben to
  the Fly Trap, pond, raceway, gardens and Victory Totem Poles.
- The new Observatory comes in both looks.
- Classic is still one switch away in Settings.

- A cleaner screen while you attack.
- Building panels, the Hatchery and other windows now fit your phone.
