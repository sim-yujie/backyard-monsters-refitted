# v2.0.0-beta.4

## Halloween
- From 28 October, for two weeks, your yard goes spooky: dark grass, and pumpkins grow where the
  mushrooms used to. The rare golden one is a glowing jack-o'-lantern.
- Pumpkin hunt: tap a pumpkin to send a worker to pick it. Every pumpkin you pick counts towards
  prizes on the Events page:
  - 10 pumpkins: 10 Shiny
  - 25 pumpkins: 25,000 of each resource
  - 50 pumpkins: 2 Mini-Pumpkins and 20 Shiny
  - 100 pumpkins: a D.A.V.E. Pumpkin and 30 Shiny
- Log in each day of Halloween to claim a treat from the 14-day calendar.

## New features
- Events page: the new Events button on your yard shows what's on now, what's coming next, the
  daily login calendar and the prizes you can claim. A badge shows when a treat is waiting.
- What's new: after an update, picture cards show you what changed. Open them again any time from
  the Account menu.
- Krallen Hunt: every month from the 15th to the end of the month (the first is 15 to 30
  November), take 100 million loot in attacks to win the champion Krallen, or raise yours one
  level, up to level 5. Raids on players, wild monster camps and tribes all count. For Town Hall 6
  and up; you have a week after the hunt ends to claim your prize.
- Free speed-ups: some treats are free speed-ups. They show up in the building panel as
  "Free (n left)" when you speed up a building.
- Champions hatch from eggs: raising a champion now gives you an egg that hatches one day later.
  Nothing speeds an egg up. Changed your mind? Cancel the egg and pick again (the new egg takes a
  full day too).
- 15 monsters have brand-new walk and attack animations: Crabatron scuttles, Fang creeps, Eye-ra
  slithers, Bolt crawls, D.A.V.E. rolls on its treads, and lots more.
- Bigger attack effects: monsters wind up and lunge into each blow, buildings flash and spark when
  hit and blow apart when they fall, damage numbers pop up bigger (and add together when lots of
  monsters hit one building), and the last blow plays in slow motion. Turn on "Reduce effects" in
  the Account menu for a calmer screen.
- Report a bug: found something broken? Use "Report a bug" in the Account menu (or under the
  sign-in box) to tell us. A screenshot is added for you, and you can take it out or add your own
  picture.
- Battle boosts in the Shop: Tower Overdrive (600 Shiny) makes your towers hit 25% harder when
  your yard is attacked. Monster Overdrive (25% more damage), Monster Defensive Buff (30% less
  damage taken) and Monster Speed Buff (50% faster) each cost 500 Shiny and help your monsters in
  your attacks and when your yard is attacked. Each lasts 24 hours; buying one again starts its 24
  hours afresh. An outpost can buy its own Tower Overdrive. Older defence replays are hidden once,
  because the game rules changed to add the boosts.
- New achievement, Huge Rage: fire the Massive putty bomb from your Catapult in an attack to earn
  it and 10 Shiny.
- Leaderboard: "Leaderboard" in the Account menu ranks every player by empire points (your yard's
  points plus what its buildings are worth) and shows each player's Map Room 2 outposts. See the
  top 100, with your own place lit up, or "You: #N" if you're further down.
- Wall upgrades in the Shop: turn every Block into stone, metal, gold or black diamond at once for
  Shiny (3 / 6 / 10 / 15 Shiny per Block for each step up). Needs Town Hall 3 / 4 / 5 / 6; outposts
  can buy them too. Blocks still being built or upgraded are finished, and damaged ones are healed.

## Improvements
- Friends list: search for a friend by name, filter by "Can help", "Online" or "Gift not sent", and
  see at a glance who you can help or send a gift to. About twice as many friends fit on a phone
  screen.
- Tidier on phones: one Menu button now holds Map, Mail, Monsters, Alerts, Goals, Defences and the
  rest, and your friends tuck away behind their own Friends button.
- Clearer battle screen on phones: the overview map and zoom fold behind one map button, and the
  top strip is a single thin row.
- On Map Room 2 on phones, Mail, My range and a new Filter button sit beside "Find a place".
- Map Room 1: online players show a green dot beside their name, and battles show as a shield (they
  attacked you) and a sword (you attacked them) with a count.
- Map Room 1 remembers how you sorted the list, and a new button flips it between highest and
  lowest first.
- Special moves tuned:
  - Fang's venom bites once a second again.
  - Bandito's whirlwind spins through enemy monsters (it never hurts buildings).
  - When no building is near, Teratorn's fireball bounces on to a wall.
  - Bolts defending from your bunkers can blink too.

## Fixes
- Bob's tips no longer cover the What's new cards. They wait until you close the cards.
- Leaving an attack before sending anything no longer leaves that yard showing "Under attack now" for several minutes. It is free again as soon as you are back on the map.
- During the guided start, the Build menu no longer shows a Build instantly button that could not be pressed while Bob is paying for the building.
- Bob's tips on the attack screen no longer sit on top of the Retreat question or the "Attack over" panel. They step aside while those are open.
- Bob's tips no longer cover other open windows, such as the Leaderboard, the Shop or Report a bug. They wait until you close the window.
- Champions now fight with the health and stats shown in their cage, including health healed while you were away, and food bonuses count in battle.
- Fixed a background server problem.
