# v2.0.0-beta.7.1

Changes:
- Your uploaded profile photo now shows to other players everywhere your critter used to: the map, the leaderboard, friends lists, the alliance window, the attack log (Defences), Map Room 1 and the Inferno player list. It also shows on your own name badge in the top right. A player with no photo, or whose photo was hidden after reports, still shows their critter.
- The Alliance button in the More menu now appears once your Observatory is finished (or if you are already in an alliance). Before, it showed on every Map Room 2 yard, even without an Observatory.

Fixes:
- The daily login pop-up no longer claims the day when you press Enter or Space for something else, such as sending a chat message. Click Claim to claim it.
- The top-right badge kept showing your critter after you uploaded a photo. It now shows the photo straight away.
