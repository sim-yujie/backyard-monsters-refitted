# v2.0.0-beta.8.2

## Repeat attack fee
- **Repeat attack fee lowered by 1M** at every camp level: now **1M** for levels 25-29,
  **1.5M** for 30-34, **2M** for 35-39 and **2.5M** for 40-44.
- Repeat attack plans are **only saved from a camp that was untouched** when you attacked.
  An attack on a camp that was already damaged keeps your old plan, and the result says
  "Repeat plan not saved: camp was already damaged."

## Attack results
- Fixed attack results **failing after the server had been running for about two days**.
  When it happened, attacks could not be worked out: saving an attack failed, and some
  attacks you had left landed **with no battle** (the monsters you flung were spent, with
  no damage or loot).
- If battles cannot be worked out again, an attack you left now **waits** until they can,
  instead of landing with no battle. The server also restarts itself.
- A new attack on a yard now **waits** while the last attack there is still being worked
  out ("The last attack on this yard is still being worked out. Try again in a minute."),
  instead of replacing it.

## Gift wishes
- When your gift wishes can't be saved, the message now **says why**.
- A wishes change that didn't reach the server is **sent again by itself** once before
  any message shows.
