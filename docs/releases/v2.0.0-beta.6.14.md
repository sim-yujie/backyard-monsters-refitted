# v2.0.0-beta.6.14

Fixes:
- New harvesters on your Map Room 2 outposts now pay their full rate as soon as they finish building. Before this fix, a harvester that finished while you were away (including the ones that come with an outpost kit) kept paying the starting minimum until you opened that outpost again, and the missed resources were never paid. The game now pays what the outpost really earned from the moment the harvester finished. Harvesters being upgraded were not affected.
- A destroyed harvester on an outpost starts earning again as soon as its repair begins, instead of waiting until you open the outpost.
