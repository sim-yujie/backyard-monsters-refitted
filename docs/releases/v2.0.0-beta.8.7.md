# v2.0.0-beta.8.7

## Monsters
- With a Hatchery Control Centre, a hatchery being upgraded no longer takes
  monsters from the queue and shows Upgrading; a monster already inside waits
  until the upgrade ends, as in Flash.

## Attacks
- Shots from Fomor, the Teratorn, D.A.V.E. and other ranged monsters land on the
  building again, not on the grass beside it. Damage is unchanged.
