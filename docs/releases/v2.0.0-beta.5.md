# v2.0.0-beta.5

## The Inferno
The underground yard from the original game is back, with Flash's rules, prices and times.
- Getting in: at Town Hall 5 a cave opens on your yard. Moloch's monsters burst out of it and
  raid you five times, at least 23 hours apart. Win or lose, every raid counts. Wild monster tribe
  raids wait until the five raids are over.
- The Descent: after the fifth raid, enter the cave. The Descent is 13 of Moloch's yards in a row.
  Do 90% damage to a yard to open the next one. You attack with monsters from your main yard. A
  yard you don't beat stays damaged for your next try.
- The burn: from the 7th Descent yard, your surface monsters burn and slowly lose health. Inferno
  monsters and Korath don't burn. (Flash only showed a warning; here the burn is real.)
- Your Inferno yard: beat the 13th yard to capture your own yard on the lava. It has its own
  resources (Bone, Coal, Sulfur and Magma), its own buildings with the Under Hall in place of the
  Town Hall, and its own shop. Shiny is shared with your main yard.
- Workers: you start with one worker. Unlike Flash, you can buy up to four more for 250, 500,
  1,000 and 2,000 Shiny.
- Eight Inferno monsters, from Spurtz to King Wormzer. Unlock and train them with Sulfur, hatch
  them with Magma in the Incubator, and keep them in Housing Bunkers. You attack from your Inferno
  yard with as many monsters as your Housing Bunkers hold.
- Transfer Monsters: from the cave, send Inferno monsters up to your main yard. It is free and one
  way. Up there they can join your attacks and defend from your Monster Bunkers.
- Inferno map: the Map button on your Inferno yard shows seven Moloch tribes near your level. A
  beaten tribe comes back after an hour. The Players tab lists other players' Inferno yards within
  7 levels of yours, to view or attack. If your Inferno yard is attacked, you get a notice with the
  damage and what was looted.
- 38 Inferno quests with Inferno resources as prizes. Five of the biggest also give Shiny (85 Shiny
  in all). Plus four Inferno achievements.
- Korath: owning an Inferno yard unlocks Korath, the fourth champion. Raise him in your Champion
  Cage. He is repainted at every level, stomp included. His three extra powers from Flash's events
  (Burn, Breath of Fire, Fists of Doom) are not part of this update.
- New on your main yard once you finish the Descent: the Quake Tower and Magma Tower, the Siege
  Works and the Siege Factory. You pay for them with Inferno resources, and your Under Hall level
  sets how many you can build.
- Siege weapons: research them in the Siege Works, build them in the Siege Factory (their window
  has Chaos Lab and Chaos Factory tabs, as in Flash), then fire one from the Siege button in an
  attack:
  - Marilyn Monstroe (Decoy) draws defending monsters to her, then blows up. She never pulls
    champions, but her blast hurts a champion in range, even one inside its cage.
  - The Loot-O-Tron 5000 (Vacuum) latches on to the Town Hall and pulls out extra loot. It needs
    the Town Hall standing.
  - Candy Jars trap defensive towers until they shoot their way out. They work on towers and
    Spurtz Cannons, not on the Map Room 3 Stronghold, bunkers or the Champion Cage.
  - Siege weapons can't be used in the Descent or on Inferno yards.
- Flash's siege weapon goals are back.
- The Inferno has its own music and collect sounds.

## New features
- Music: the game has music again, in your yard and map rooms, in attacks and when you are raided.
  Your yard has a new tune. Turn music and sound effects on or off in the Account menu or with the
  speaker button by the zoom buttons. Music starts off on a brand-new yard, as in Flash.
- Flash's collect sounds are back: a pop as each resource ball leaves a harvester and as it lands.
- Modern or Classic: Account Settings has one "Look and sound" choice. Modern (the default) shows
  the new art and effects. Classic brings back the original Flash look wherever Flash had one:
  buildings, monster and champion pictures, battle effects and the old yard tune.
- Painted windows: game windows get painted frames, riveted steel in Modern and Flash-style
  parchment in Classic.
- Quality: choose High, Medium or Low in Account Settings. Medium and Low load smaller monster
  pictures and lighter music, for slow internet. If your connection looks slow, the game suggests
  Low once. It never switches by itself.
- Tower shots: the Tesla, Laser, Railgun, Cannon, Sniper Tower and Aerial Defense Tower have new
  painted shots and hits. Account Settings has a new Game effects section: Screen shake on or off,
  and Effects Full or Low.
- Status icons: a monster that is poisoned, burning, enraged, puttied or loot-boosted shows small
  round stickers over its health bar.
- New Town Hall pictures for levels 1 to 10.
- Champions repainted: Gorgo, Drull and Fomor at levels 2 to 6. Krallen's look now follows her
  level (look 1 at levels 1-2, look 2 at levels 3-4, look 3 at level 5), and all three looks are
  repainted, with a shadow under her.
- Hatchery Clear all: each hatchery queue and the Hatchery Control Centre queue has a Clear all
  button. It asks first, then empties the queue and gives back the goo as if you cancelled each
  monster. The monster already hatching carries on.
- New resource icons: each resource shows as a whole pile.
- Discord: "Join the Discord" in the Account menu, and a Discord link on the sign-in screen and the
  website.

## Improvements
- Fewer words, more pictures: amounts, costs and stats show as painted icons with numbers on the
  top bar, in building windows, in the Shop and on the attack screen. Gains show in green with a
  +, losses in red with a −.
- Top bar: workers show as free/total (like 3/5), and a full silo shows its full mark. Gains and
  costs float up beside the top bar.
- Building windows: the level, cost and time sit on one line, with what you gain beside Upgrade.
  The Town Hall shows next level's unlocks as building pictures marked +1 or NEW.
- Hatcheries: with two or more hatcheries, the hatching screen has one tab per hatchery instead of
  one long list. The game remembers your last tab.
- Champion Cage: one click on the cage opens straight on your champion, laid out like Flash's cage
  window. Upgrade and repair are on a second tab.
- Raising a champion gives it to you straight away, like the original game. The egg and its 1-day
  wait from beta 4 are gone. Champions can no longer be juiced, and without a Champion Chamber you
  can't swap your champion for another type.
- Champion Chamber: one click opens straight on your champions, one card each, with Freeze or
  Thaw. Upgrade and repair are on a Building tab, with a red dot when the chamber needs repair.
- Map Room: a tribe camp shows how much health it has left, not the damage done.
- Attack screen: shorter text, a wall icon for damage, and a shorter Retreat question.
- The WILD MONSTER ALERT window's border pulses red.
- Bob's guided start: one short line per bubble, led by a picture of the button to press.
- Pictures are kept in your browser for a week, so the game loads faster next time.

## Fixes
- Big painted monsters (such as D.A.V.E. and Zafreeti) could show as a black box, or not at all,
  on phones and computers whose graphics can't hold such a large picture. The game now loads a
  smaller copy that fits.
- New players could get stuck in the guided start when the level-up box covered the Build button.
  The box now waits until the guide is done.
- In the guided start, Bob now pays for the whole building, as he says. Before, your own Twigs and
  Pebbles still dropped to 0.
- At the practice camp, Bob's hand no longer covers the card, and the pin goes away after you win.
- Halloween pumpkins no longer sink into wall blocks.
- A champion walking at the back of its cage is no longer drawn behind the cage's fence.
- The Blueprint view unlocks only once the Yard Planner is finished.
- The "buildings destroyed" count after an attack no longer counts walls.
- The Retreat question's damage now keeps up while the fight goes on.
- Monster stats no longer run off the edge of their card.
- The Build menu's "pick a spot" line stays on screen in short windows.

## Coming later: 3D towers
The Cannon, Sniper Tower, Laser, Aerial Defense, Railgun, Spurtz Cannon, Black Spurtz Cannon and the
Inferno towers are being rebuilt in 3D, with turrets that turn to aim. This is still in progress
and switched off for players in beta 5. We plan to bring it in beta 6 or 7.
