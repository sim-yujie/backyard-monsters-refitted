# v2.0.0-beta.8

Full details, with pictures, on Patreon: https://www.patreon.com/TheMonsterLab/posts/beta-8-is-live-175786472

## Inferno art (Modern look)
- Now 3D: Coal Extractor, Sulfur Swirler, Magma Pump, Strongbox, Incubator, Academy.
- Newly painted: Under Hall levels 4-6, Compound, Resource Pod, Inferno Cavern (on your main yard).
- All 8 Inferno monsters repainted: Spurtz, Zagnoid, Malphus, Valgos, Balthazar, Grokus, Sabnox, King Wormzer.
- Main yard: Siege Factory and Siege Works now in 3D.
- Fortification art redrawn at twice the resolution.
- Classic is unchanged.

## Alliances
- New **Trade tab**: swap one resource for another with an alliance mate.
  - Each side receives 90% of what the other sends.
  - A swap is at most 10% of each player's own storage cap for that resource.
  - Requests last 48 hours. 1 open request at a time, 3 requests a day, 1 completed swap
    a day.
  - An accepted request leaves Mates' requests.
  - After a swap, your request box shows who you swapped with and what you got.
  - A grayed-out Accept button says why you can't accept.
- **Goals** have their own tab.
  - Leader and Officers can remove any unfinished goal. A removed goal's type can't be set
    again that week.
  - "Hold outposts at the end of the week" goal: can't be set at a target the alliance
    already holds.
  - "Take over outposts" goal: takeovers from your own alliance's members don't count.
  - "Rebuild outposts with Starter Kits" goal: counts only destroyed or empty outposts.
- **Constellations**: stars 5-10 of a constellation cost double.
  - A full Midas' Council constellation costs 1,080 points (was 600).
  - Conquest Horde constellations cost twice the Midas' Council points; Fortress Legion
    constellations cost four times.
  - Stars already lit stay lit. Unspent points are kept.
- Switching alliance type no longer refunds spent points twice.

## Map Room 2 attacks
- **Repeat attack**: if your yards in Flinger range are short of monsters, it moves them in
  from your other yards first.
  - The window lists each move, each in its own box; the list scrolls inside the window.
  - The button reads Move and attack.
  - If you are short of monsters or housing, it shows how many, per monster type and per
    yard.
  - A bar showing your marching army runs while the fight is calculated (at least 5
    seconds), with a count for every monster type.
  - The window is laid out like the Attack window, with the camp's picture, level and
    damage %.
  - Monsters show as tiles with their count, your champion with its level, the catapult as
    resource piles.
- **Repeat attack result**: win or loss, damage, loot and takeover cost, with Take over,
  Watch and Close.

## Battles
- Attack dock: Army, Catapult and siege weapon (Decoy, Vacuum or Jars) are tabs in one row.
- An armed bomb stays armed when you switch tabs.
- Army tab: the new "Auto-load last army" checkbox refills the Flinger with your last army
  after every fling.
- Attack again removed from the Attack over panel. Use Repeat attack on the map to attack
  again.
- Champions frozen in the Champion Chamber are no longer listed in the Army tab.
- Damage numbers: red when your side loses health, white when the other side does. Heals
  stay green.
- Rezghul's orb is repainted in both looks (sea-green, was Flash's orange fireball).
- Monsters Rezghul raises climb out of the ground and turn gray.

## Champions
- Each habit is Active or Unproven.
- A habit can become Active after at least 8 attacks in its Mode have tested it and 3
  consecutive tests have supported it.
- 8 consecutive tests that contradict a habit make it Unproven again.
- The Champion tab marks which habit your last attack Learned or Faded.

## Friends
- Your builds, upgrades and fortifications in your main yard show how many times friends
  have helped (e.g. 2/5).
- Visiting a friend: each building you can speed up shows a wrench, a ring and how much
  time your help saves.
- **Help all** applies every available speed-up on the yard in one tap.
- Visiting a friend: name, tools count and Help all are on one bar.
- Previous and Next friend arrows are beside Map. Next shows how many remaining friends in
  the list you can still help.

## Yard
- **Upgrade preview** (Modern only): when Upgrade is available in a building's window, its
  next level appears on your yard as a blue ghost.
- **Reset clock**: the hourglass beside "?" lists the daily, weekly, weekend event and
  Krallen Hunt resets.
- Protection shows as a shield with the time remaining.
- Running boosts share one button with a count. Tower Overdrive, Monster Overdrive and
  monster buffs now show as running boosts.
- Blocks can be upgraded at any Town Hall level (was Stone TH3, Metal TH4, Gold TH5, Black
  Diamond TH6, Inferno Granite UH3, Forged Steel UH4).
- Any wall can be upgraded on its own from its pop-up. A single wall upgrade finishes at
  once and needs no worker.
- Town Hall unlock list: Champion Cage added, Inferno towers removed.
- The Under Hall lists the main-yard buildings it unlocks.

## Thanksgiving
- Golden turkeys are now 1 in 10 turkeys, down from 1 in 4.
- Turkeys still come about once an hour.
- Every turkey you catch still counts for the turkey ladder.

## Yard Planner
- As in Flash, a built Yard Planner in a yard unlocks layout slots 2-10, tower ranges, dead
  zones, Find, Compare, Yard picture and selecting multiple walls.
- Without one, you get 1 layout slot. Outposts always have 1 slot. Saved layouts are kept.
- Walls are drawn as a line: tap the corners, double-tap to finish. Green walls are placed,
  red walls are blocked, gray walls exceed the number you have.
- Buildings you carry show a green or red placement box.
- Inferno: the Layouts button now appears.

## Leaderboard and profiles
- Empire leaderboard: each Map Room 2 outpost adds 1 point for every 3 build or fortify
  levels of its buildings.
- Leaderboard rows show how many outposts a player holds.
- For tied empire scores, the player who reached their main yard score first ranks higher.
- Achievement counts are no longer shown on profiles or map cards.
- Map cards no longer show another player's main yard empire value.

## Fixes
- Reloading on a phone no longer keeps two copies of the game's pictures in memory.
- iPhone: the yard no longer sticks in the top-left corner after a pinch zoom.
- Map Room 2: zooming out no longer freezes the map for up to 1.5 seconds.
- Shop: the list no longer jumps every second.
- Move Monsters: Fill no longer brings up a scroll bar.
- Classic: Shop and Events keep their list on short screens.
- Alliance window: bigger tabs on touch screens.
- Leaving an alliance asks Leave or Stay.
- Moloch's cave: if a cave stage rose while you were away, its warning and raid now play
  when you return.
- Game pictures load as plain images, using less memory on phones and tablets.
- Moving the Champion Cage in the Yard Planner no longer leaves your champion behind; it
  walks to the new cage.
- Hatch tab: when all the monsters you add go in, no "Added ..." message covers the
  hatchery cards any more. If only some go in, it still says how many and why.
