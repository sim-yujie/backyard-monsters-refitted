# v2.0.0-beta.8

Full details, with pictures, on Patreon: https://www.patreon.com/TheMonsterLab/posts/beta-8-is-live-175786472

## New Modern art: the Inferno in 3D
- In the Modern look, the Inferno's **Coal Extractor, Sulfur Swirler, Magma Pump,
  Strongbox, Incubator and Academy** are now 3D.
- **All 8 Inferno monsters are repainted**: Spurtz, Zagnoid, Malphus, Valgos, Balthazar,
  Grokus, Sabnox and King Wormzer.
- Newly painted: **Under Hall levels 4-6**, the Compound, the Resource Pod and the Inferno
  Cavern on your main yard.
- The **Siege Factory and Siege Works** on your main yard are now 3D.
- **Fortification art** is redrawn at twice the resolution.
- Classic is unchanged.

## Trade with your alliance
- A new **Trade tab** in the Alliance window: swap one resource for another with an
  alliance mate. **Each side receives 90%** of what the other sends.
- A swap is at most **10% of each player's own storage cap** for that resource.
- Requests last 48 hours. You can have **1 open request** at a time, post **3 requests a
  day** and complete **1 swap a day**.
- Accepted requests are removed from **Mates' requests**. After a swap, your request box
  shows who you swapped with and what you got.
- A grayed-out Accept button says why you can't accept.

## Alliance goals and stars
- **Goals have their own tab.** The leader and officers can remove any unfinished goal; a
  removed goal's type can't be set again that week.
- "Hold outposts at the end of the week" can't be set at a target the alliance already
  holds. "Take over outposts" no longer counts takeovers from your own alliance's members.
  "Rebuild outposts with Starter Kits" counts only destroyed or empty outposts.
- **Stars 5-10 of a constellation cost double.** A full Midas' Council constellation now
  costs 1,080 points (was 600). Conquest Horde constellations cost twice the Midas'
  Council points, and Fortress Legion constellations four times.
- Stars already lit stay lit, and unspent points are kept.
- Switching alliance type no longer refunds spent points twice.

## Repeat attack moves your monsters
- If your yards in Flinger range are short of monsters, **Repeat attack moves them in from
  your other yards first**. The window lists each move in its own box, and the button
  reads **Move and attack**.
- If you are short of monsters or housing, the window shows the **shortages**, per
  monster type and per yard.
- The window is **laid out like the Attack window**: the camp's picture, level and damage
  %, your monsters as tiles with their count, your champion with its level, and the
  catapult as resource piles. The list of moves scrolls inside the window.
- While the fight is calculated (at least 5 seconds), a **bar shows your army marching**,
  with a count for every monster type.
- The **result** shows win or loss, damage, loot and takeover cost, with Take over, Watch
  and Close.

## Attack dock and battle looks
- **Army, Catapult and your siege weapon** (Decoy, Vacuum or Jars) are tabs in one row. An
  armed bomb stays armed when you switch tabs.
- **Auto-load last army**, a new checkbox in the Army tab, refills the Flinger with your
  last army after every fling.
- Attack again is gone from the Attack over panel: use **Repeat attack** on the map to
  attack again.
- Champions frozen in the Champion Chamber are no longer listed in the Army tab.
- **Damage numbers** are red when your side loses health and white when the other side
  does. Heals stay green.
- **Rezghul's orb** is repainted in both looks: sea-green, where Flash had an orange
  fireball. Monsters Rezghul raises climb out of the ground and turn gray.

## Champion habits: Active and Unproven
- Each habit is now **Active** or **Unproven**.
- A habit can become Active after at least **8 attacks in its Mode** have tested it and
  **3 tests in a row** have supported it. **8 tests in a row** that contradict it make it
  Unproven again.
- The Champion tab marks which habit your last attack **Learned** or **Faded**.

## Helping friends
- Your builds, upgrades and fortifications in your main yard show **how many times friends
  have helped** (for example 2/5).
- When you visit a friend, each building you can speed up shows a wrench, a ring and
  **how much time your help saves**.
- **Help all** applies every available speed-up on the yard in one tap. The friend's name,
  your tools count and Help all sit on one bar.
- **Previous and Next friend** arrows are beside Map. Next shows how many of the remaining
  friends in the list you can still help.

## Yard Planner, as in Flash
- A **built Yard Planner** in a yard unlocks layout slots 2-10, tower ranges, dead zones,
  Find, Compare, Yard picture and selecting multiple walls. Without one, you get 1 layout
  slot. Outposts always have 1 slot. Saved layouts are kept.
- **Walls are drawn as a line**: tap the corners and double-tap to finish. Green walls are
  placed, red walls are blocked, and gray walls exceed the number you have.
- Buildings you carry show a green or red placement box.
- In the Inferno, the Layouts button now appears.

## Your yard
- **Upgrade preview** (Modern only): when Upgrade is available in a building's window, its
  next level appears on your yard as a blue ghost.
- **Reset clock**: the hourglass beside "?" lists the daily, weekly, weekend event and
  Krallen Hunt resets.
- **Protection** shows as a shield with the time remaining.
- **Running boosts** share one button with a count. Tower Overdrive, Monster Overdrive and
  monster buffs now show there too.
- **Blocks can be upgraded at any Town Hall level** (was Stone TH3, Metal TH4, Gold TH5,
  Black Diamond TH6, Inferno Granite UH3, Forged Steel UH4).
- **Any wall can be upgraded on its own** from its pop-up. A single wall upgrade finishes
  at once and needs no worker.
- The Town Hall unlock list now includes the Champion Cage and no longer lists the Inferno
  towers. The Under Hall lists the main-yard buildings it unlocks.

## Empire leaderboard and profiles
- Each **Map Room 2 outpost** adds 1 point to your empire score for every 3 build or
  fortify levels of its buildings.
- Leaderboard rows show **how many outposts** a player holds.
- For tied empire scores, the player who reached their main yard score first ranks higher.
- Achievement counts are no longer shown on profiles or map cards, and map cards no
  longer show another player's main yard empire value.

## Thanksgiving
- **Golden turkeys are now 1 in 10** turkeys, down from 1 in 4.
- Turkeys still come about once an hour, and every turkey you catch still counts for the
  turkey ladder.

## Improvements
- **Reloading on a phone** no longer keeps two copies of the game's pictures in memory,
  and game pictures load as plain images, using **less memory** on phones and tablets.
- On **iPhone**, the yard no longer sticks in the top-left corner after a **pinch zoom**.
- **Zooming out on Map Room 2** no longer freezes the map for up to 1.5 seconds.
- The **Shop list** no longer jumps every second.
- In **Move Monsters**, **Fill** no longer brings up a scroll bar.
- In **Classic**, the Shop and Events keep their list on short screens.
- The **Alliance window** has bigger tabs on touch screens, and **leaving an alliance**
  asks Leave or Stay.
- **Moloch's cave**: if a cave stage rose while you were away, its warning and raid now play
  when you return.
- Moving the **Champion Cage** in the Yard Planner no longer leaves your champion behind; it
  walks to the new cage.
- In the **Hatch tab**, when every monster you pick is added, no "Added ..." message covers
  the hatchery cards any more. If only some are added, it still says how many and why.
