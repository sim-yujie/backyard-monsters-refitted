# v2.0.0-beta.6.15

Fixes:
- Eye-ras now hurt monsters when they explode, as in the original game. An Eye-ra defending from a Monster Bunker runs at the nearest attacking monster and blows up, damaging every ground monster close by. Before this fix, defending Eye-ras did nothing to attackers.
- When you attack with Eye-ras, their blast now also damages the defending ground monsters and the defending champion close by, not only buildings. Flying monsters are not hurt by the blast, and walls in the way are still hit first.
- The same rules apply in Map Room 1, Map Room 2 and the Descent.
