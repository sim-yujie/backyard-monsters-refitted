# v2.0.0-beta.3

## Friends
- Add friends: search for a player by name (your alliance mates are listed there too), or press
  "Add friend" on their yard in the Map Room or on an attack message in your mailbox. They accept,
  and you are friends.
- A friends bar along the bottom of your yard shows your friends' portraits. Click one to visit.
- Visit a friend's yard to look around.
- Help a friend: speed up a building they are building or upgrading, or repair their damaged
  buildings. Each help earns you a small reward. There is a daily limit on how many times you can
  help.
- Gifts: send each friend one free resource gift a day. Collect the gifts friends send you from the
  Gifts tab, and send one back.
- Privacy settings: block a player, hide when you are online, or turn off friend requests.
- Attacking a friend asks you to confirm first.

## New features
- Defence replays: on Map Room 2, open "Defences" in your yard to watch the last 20 attacks on your
  yard, at 1x, 2x or 4x speed, with a skip button.
- Wormzers tunnel underground like in the original game: they dig in, can't be seen or shot while
  they travel, and pop up to bite.
- Wormzers sent out from your bunkers tunnel too, so your own defenders sneak up on attackers.
- New shots for Fomor (magenta orb), Zafreeti (sea-green healing orb) and Vorg, instead of the same
  orange fireball for everyone.
- Eye-ra now goes off in a proper fiery explosion with smoke and a scorch mark.
- Bigger hits feel bigger: Korath's quake shakes the screen and Wormzers burst out of the ground with
  a flash.
- Special moves researched in the Monster Lab look the part: D.A.V.E. fires twin rockets with smoke,
  Teratorns' fireballs hop on from building to building like in the original game, and Bandito's
  whirlwind flashes.
- Move to a random spot: on Map Room 2, you can use the Map Room to move your main yard to a random
  new spot on the map, free, once every 7 days (your outposts are lost, the game warns you first).
- Monsters now walk or fly off the edge of the yard at the end of an attack instead of just
  vanishing.

## Improvements
- Building with every worker busy: pick the spot first, then choose how to free a worker; the
  building goes up on that spot as soon as one is free.
- The yard view no longer jumps away after you place a building, and build timers count down
  steadily.
- Tribe raids now come at least 3 hours apart.
- "Skip tips" now closes all of Bob's tips at once.
- The account menu no longer sits on top of the zoom buttons.
- Clearer wording on the notice shown when your housing is too full.
- The friends bar and friends list fit better on phones and smaller screens, and no longer cover the dock buttons.
- The friends bar now pages properly on wide screens when you have many friends.

## Fixes
- Eye-ra blasts now always damage the building they reach (before, about 2 in 5 blasts did nothing).
- Towers now aim at flying monsters where you actually see them.
- Ichi and Fang face the way they walk.
- Attacking a Map Room 2 yard that sits on a map edge no longer offers double the monsters and then
  refuses the result.
- A Wormzer's dirt mound is now drawn under bunker roofs instead of on top of them.
- Battles with lots of Banditos no longer stutter when they spin at the same time.
- Other players can no longer see when wild monsters are due to raid your yard.
- Fixed a background server problem.
