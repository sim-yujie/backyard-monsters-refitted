# v2.0.0-beta.8.5

## Battles
- Ranged monsters and Fomor now attack a building from a little closer than their
  full range, so they stop at a sensible distance. Their rockets fly to the near
  edge of the building.
- Krallen now finishes off a harvester she has drained, as in Flash.

## Outposts
- Housing that finished building while an outpost was closed is now counted when
  you open the outpost.

## Monster Locker
- You can no longer start a monster unlock while the Locker is being upgraded.

## Screens
- Chat fits a sideways phone and short screens: the open chat box no longer runs
  under the left-hand buttons.
- The account menu stays on the screen in a friend's yard.

## Privacy
- The privacy page now says the game server is in Singapore and that Cloudflare
  serves the game pictures.
