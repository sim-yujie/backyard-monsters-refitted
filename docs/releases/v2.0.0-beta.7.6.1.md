# v2.0.0-beta.7.6.1

Fixes:
- Hatch tab: the hatchery cards no longer run into each other. "Waiting for housing" is now "Needs housing" ("Full" on a phone) and always shows in full.
- Hatch tab: the empty queue boxes are back, so you can see how many more batches fit.
- Hatch tab: the housing numbers stay inside their box. When housing is full, the warning has its own line and the numbers go on the line under it.
- Hatch tab: the window is only as tall as what is in it, so there is no big empty space under the monsters any more. Its top and tabs stay where they were.
- Hatch tab: choosing how many to add no longer moves the Add button or the monsters.
- Hatch tab: the "+20" on a batch card is no longer partly hidden behind the monster's picture.
- Hatch tab: the line under Add is shorter ("108 fit in housing; 37 wait for room") and no longer cut off.
- Hatch tab: "Details" sits beside the monster's name instead of alone in the corner.
- Hatch tab: long monster names (Slimeattikus, Octo-ooze, Crabatron) are no longer cut with "…" on a computer or an iPad.
- Hatch tab: the number box and Max fit inside the panel on an iPad on its side, and the box shows three-digit counts in full.
- Hatch tab: without a Hatchery Control Centre, the tab fits a 1366x768 screen with no scrolling again.
- Hatch tab on a phone: the queue boxes are shorter, and a hatchery tab's number and count no longer touch.
- Hatch tab: the housing total under the queue now reads "After: 18,720 / 2,160", so it is clear it is the housing once everything queued has hatched, not the housing now.
