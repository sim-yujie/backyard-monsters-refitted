# v2.0.0-beta.7.4

Changes:
- Weekend events: the event is now a card in the Events window instead of a bar on the yard. Check in once per event day for 50 Glimmers, on the card or in its pop-up. A missed day is gone. The old 25 and 75 Glimmers payments are removed.
- Daily quests: the day's last quest pays 5 Glimmers.
- Krallen's loot aura: a level 3, 4 or 5 Krallen has the aura only after you loot 3x the hunt goal and claim that mark on the hunt card. It then lasts until the end of the next hunt. Ordinary monsters near her loot up to x2 / x2.8 / x3.6 at her levels 3 / 4 / 5; resource hunters and other champions x1.25 / x1.45 / x1.65.
- Krallen Hunt goals, by her level when the hunt begins: 60M / 90M / 150M / 250M / 300M. November's hunt is unchanged.
- Krallen Hunt penalty: if you owned Krallen when a hunt began and end it below half the goal, she loses one level; a level 1 Krallen leaves and must be won again. The hunt card shows a red mark at half the goal. November has no penalty.
- Flinger: after every fling the counts go back to 0, as in Flash. Load last army brings the batch back.
- Profile: photo, bio and critter are now in Settings, Account, with a Preview button. The Profile button is gone from the account menu.
- Fewer notice boxes: 24 boxes that only repeated what the screen already shows are removed. Glimmers gained or spent now float as +N or −N beside the Glimmers number. While an attack loads, the Attack button is greyed and pulses.

Fixes:
- Map Room 2 no longer counts outpost income twice in the totals it shows. No resources were ever lost.
- The "stayed in your harvesters: storage is full" box is removed.
- In a battle, the Catapult window no longer gets scrollbars that appear and disappear.
