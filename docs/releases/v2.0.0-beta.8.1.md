# v2.0.0-beta.8.1

## Repeat attack fee
- **Repeat attack now costs putty**, by the camp's level: **2M** for levels 25-29,
  **2.5M** for 30-34, **3M** for 35-39 and **3.5M** for 40-44.
- The fee is taken **when the attack starts**, win or lose. If you don't have enough putty
  (fee plus any putty catapult shots), the attack doesn't start and nothing is spent.
- The Repeat attack window shows the fee under the catapult. Attacks you play yourself
  stay free.

## General Store in the Inferno
- You can now **build a General Store in the Inferno**: Under Hall level 1, one per
  Inferno, 1,080 Bone and 720 Coal. Its panel opens the shop, and a worker bought there
  joins your Inferno workers.
- In the Modern look it is a new Inferno shop with its own damaged and destroyed looks. In
  Classic it is the Flash store, with a flame build button like the other Inferno
  buildings.

## Building walls
- When you draw a **wall line**, the outlines of nearby buildings now **stay visible**
  after the first corner, in both yards.
- In the Inferno, those outlines are **easier to see on lava**: a light line with a dark
  edge.

## Empire leaderboard
- Points an outpost had already added to your main yard score are **no longer counted a
  second time** in your empire score.
