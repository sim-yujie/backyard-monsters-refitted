# v2.0.0-beta.6.3

Chat:
- Settings > Social now lists the players you muted in chat, each with an Unmute button. Their messages show again straight away.
- The Muted and Blocked lists have their own search box and scroll, and Unmute and Unblock ask you to confirm first.

Windows:
- Tabs now stay at the top of every window while you scroll, and every row of tabs fills its window.
- On small phone screens the Hatchery, Friends and help windows give more room to their lists.

Speed-ups:
- A free 1-hour or 2-hour speed-up can now finish a build or upgrade that has less time left. The button says how much of it goes unused before you tap.
