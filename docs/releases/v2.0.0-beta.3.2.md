# v2.0.0-beta.3.2

- Yard layouts now apply when the Trojan horse is waiting outside your yard. Before, the planner's
  checklist said the horse was "outside the yard" and Apply stayed blocked.
- Other players can no longer see your layout, your building levels or your traps. The Yard Planner
  only opens on your own yards. When someone looks at your yard, their game is not sent your
  traps, the real levels of your buildings or your Shiny count, and the building panel shows
  "Level: Hidden". Someone who really attacks you still fights your full defence, traps included.
- Champions now fight at the health shown in their cage, including the health they healed while you
  were away. Food bonuses now count in battle too, for health, damage, speed and range, the same as
  on the cage card. The cage panel now says "<name>'s health" and "Max health" so the two are easy
  to tell apart.

Because of the champion change, defence replays recorded before this update can no longer be
played. They show "Replay not available after a game update". New attacks record replays as usual.
