# v2.0.0-beta.9.1

## Attacks
- The extra Goo from wild monster tribes (+25% since patch 6.9) steps down:
  **+20%** from this patch, **+10%** from 30 Nov 05:00 UTC, and none from
  2 Dec 05:00 UTC. Map Room 2 camps' own loot bonus, Plunder, Inferno tribes
  and outposts are unchanged.
- Catapult: twig and pebble bombs now blast a circle on the ground, the same
  shape as the putty bomb's ring and the Flinger's ring. It covers the same
  area as before, a little wider and flatter on screen. The aiming ring, where
  a bomb may land and where the bombs rain down all follow the new circle.

## Frontier Pass
- A new Frontier Pass starts at the next daily reset (00:00 UTC) after you join
  Map Room 2 and runs 10 reset days from there. Until then the card shows a
  "Starts in" countdown, both rewards say "Ready soon" and there is no Go
  button.
- A pass already running loses no time: it now ends at the next reset after
  its old end, so its day 10 can last longer.
- Each day's takeover and kit stamps sit stacked on that day's dot.

## Fixes
- Map Room 2: the **Your outpost** card fits the screen again (it ran off the
  screen in beta 9).
- **Your yards** list keeps its scroll place when you switch from yard to yard.
- Hatch tab with a Hatchery Control Centre: the housing bar shows the monsters
  you picked, in their own colour and as "+N", before you press **Add**, and
  turns amber past your room. Level 1's three locked queue boxes stay while
  you change the count.
- The same housing bar shows the queue's blue "+N" only when the queue adds
  something (no more "+0"), and on a phone the number no longer sticks out of
  the box.
