# v2.0.0-beta.7.10

Fixes:
- Champions: Gorgo, Drull, Korath and Krallen now hit a building only from right beside it (one tile). Before, they could hit buildings from their full range, up to 9 tiles away for some. Their range against monsters, and Korath's quake, are unchanged.
- Fomor and the ranged champions (Teratorn, Rezghul, Sabnox) are unchanged.
- Replays of attacks from before this patch can no longer be played.
