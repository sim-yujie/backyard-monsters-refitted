# v2.0.0-beta.7.8

Fixes:
- Quests: monsters that finish hatching while you are attacking now count toward daily hatch quests.
- Champion habits: Fomor now learns from the monsters it backs: how much of your monsters' damage to buildings comes from inside its rage ring, and how many of the hurt monsters it follows survive. Before, its habits hardly changed. Its learned habits and attack counts are kept.
- Champion window: the attack count under Habits now counts only attacks fought in a Mode. Before, it also counted every attack from before Modes.
- Older phones: a phone with 4 GB of memory or less now starts on Medium quality, with lighter monster pictures. Before, some old phones drew monsters as green squares. The music plays as normal; it only waits for you to turn it on if you pick Medium yourself. A quality you already picked in Settings is kept.
- When the game fails to draw its pictures, it now sends us a short report with your device's graphics details, so we can fix it. The Privacy Policy lists what is sent.
