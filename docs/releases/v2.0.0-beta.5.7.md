# v2.0.0-beta.5.7

Chat:
- Reporting a chat message works again. Before, the report button failed and the report was lost.
- Unmuting a player now sticks. Before, an unmute made while chat was reconnecting could undo itself.
- The word filter now also catches common Filipino swear words, in chat, mail, alliance text and names.

Staying connected (players reported it):
- You are no longer disconnected while playing: Inferno attacks, monster transfers, helping friends,
  gifts and truce requests now count as playing.
- Opening the game or coming back to its tab near the end of your protection no longer disconnects you
  before you can tap "Stay protected?".
- The messages now say what happened: "Tap within 0:45 to stay connected", and "Protection ended"
  if it runs out.
- Chatting and sending mail keep you connected but do not extend your protection.

Attacks:
- Fixed: an attack on a wild monster camp could end with "We encountered an error while saving" and
  lose its result if another player opened that camp while you were fighting it.

Game team:
- Game team messages in chat now carry a green GM badge, so you always know when it's really us.
- Players who break the chat rules can now get a warning, a chat pause or a ban, with the reason
  shown in the game.
