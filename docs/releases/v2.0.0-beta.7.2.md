# v2.0.0-beta.7.2

Changes:
- Gift all: the Friends window has a Gift all button. Each friend gets their first wish; friends with no wish get the resource you pick. You see the total cost before anything is sent. If you are short of a resource, a friend gets their next wish you can afford, or is skipped and named in the summary.
- The Friends window now stays open after you send a gift, so you can gift several friends in a row.
- The gift picker greys out resources you don't have enough of.
- Announcements can now give Glimmers as well as Shiny. After you claim, a Shop button opens the decorations.
- Chat lines show each player's picture: their uploaded photo, or their critter.

Fixes:
- Hatching on phones: tapping a monster, +, +5 or − no longer scrolls the list back to the top.
- On phones held upright, the chat no longer runs under the Events button.
- The Build window's Glimmers count now updates straight away when you receive Glimmers.
