# v2.0.0-beta.6

## Map Room 2: a new look (Modern)
- The map is one painted landscape: grass, hills, rocky cliffs, forests, rock fields,
  goo marsh, beaches and surf along the coast, and rivers along cell edges.
- The camera is tilted, so you look across the map instead of straight down. Don't like
  it? Switch the map flat for your account.
- Hexes are pointy-topped, in Modern and Classic.
- Land matters: forest, rock and marsh cells change a cell's defence and income. Cells
  next to your own cells defend better (+5% to +15%), shown as a shield in the cell's
  pop-up.
- Players' land has glowing borders and name tags. Cells you can't see are hidden under
  clouds on Quality High, and dark on Medium and Low.
- On Quality High, water moves, foam runs along the shore, clouds drift overhead and
  gulls fly past. Medium keeps the moving water and foam. Low is still.
- Classic keeps the original Flash map art, and gets the same moving water and clouds.

## 3D buildings (Modern)
- Most yard buildings are rebuilt in 3D, with moving parts: Twig Snapper, Pebble
  Shiner, Putty Squisher, Goo Factory, Flinger, Silo, Juicer, Yard Planner, Map Room,
  General Store, Hatchery, Housing, Hatchery Control Center, Monster Baiter, Academy,
  Catapult, Champion Cage, Monster Lab, Champion Chamber, the Quake Tower, Booby Traps
  and Heavy Traps, and wall blocks.
- Every one has its own damaged and destroyed look.
- Traps have a new explosion.
- The Sniper Tower and Cannon have new shapes, closer to the original.
- On the Inferno yard: a new Incubator Control Centre on a lava pool, and new Inferno
  blocks.
- The Build menu shows the new pictures.
- Classic keeps the original Flash buildings.

## New features
- **Quests.** A 7-day login card, 5 daily quests and 3 weekly quests, all paying Shiny.
  One free swap a day for a daily quest you don't like. Find them on your main yard.
- **Monster Lab abilities.** Four new upgrades in the Monster Lab:
  - Pokey, **Swarm**: when 5 or more Pokeys attack the same target, each one attacks
    faster.
  - Octo-ooze, **Weakening Ooze**: whatever kills an Octo-ooze deals less damage for
    5 seconds.
  - Ichi, **Thorns**: Ichi reflects part of the damage it takes back at whatever hit it.
  - Crabatron, **Critical Hits**: Crabatron's hits have a chance to do double damage.
  - Damage numbers in a fight show critical hits and reduced hits.
- **Decorations for Shiny.** The original game's decorations are on sale again, in the
  Build menu's Decorations tab, sorted into Flash's five sub-tabs.
- **The Juicer show.** Monsters walk into the Juicer, get blended, and goo and giblets
  spurt out.
- **Wild tribe camps rebuilt.** Every Map Room tribe camp has a new hand-made yard at
  every level, and the tribes are rebalanced: Legionnaire is the weakest, Dreadnaut the
  strongest, Kozu the richest.

## Sound
- The original attack sounds are back: tower shots, monster deaths, bombs, Wormzer, the
  Bunker, Candy Jars, the Decoy and the Vacuum.
- Loot flies out of buildings during an attack as orbs, with "+" numbers.
- Separate volume sliders for **Music** and **Sound effects**.

## Improvements
- **Font.** Settings ▸ Look and sound ▸ Font: Game font, Clear, or Extra readable.
- **World chat on or off.** Settings ▸ Social ▸ World chat. Off hides the chat box.
- **Tower range.** Hover over one of your own defences to see how far it reaches.
- **One "More" button.** Quests, Layout, Goals, alerts, Defences and Mail fan out from
  one round button. The bottom row is tidier, most of all on phones.
- **"New version" card.** When an update goes live, an open game tab tells you, so
  you can reload.
- **Yard Planner.** The bottom bar is now painted buttons: Undo, Redo, Select, Box, Find,
  Upgrade walls and Re-arm traps. Mirror, Align and Distribute are gone.
- **Upgrade walls.** No need to select walls first. Press Upgrade walls in the planner
  or in a wall's pop-up, pick a level, then pick walls yourself or use Select all or
  Affordable.
- **Account menu.** Your level now shows on your account button, and the menu's items
  sit in three groups of painted buttons. Settings has three tabs: Account, Look and
  sound, and Social.
- **Support the game.** A new item in the account menu with Patreon and Ko-fi links, and
  a rare reminder you can switch off.
- **Shiny Lock.** The Shiny counter shows a padlock instead of 0 while Shiny Lock is on.
- **Tribe camp health bars.** A damaged camp in Map Room 2 shows a health bar over it,
  and heals 12 hours after the last attack.
- **Event pumpkins.** A pick that reaches a reward shows a notice with a Claim button.

## Fixes
- The Modern yard music plays again in Chrome, Edge and Firefox.
- Leaving a battle no longer makes it look as if it never happened.
- Attacks on wild tribes, the Descent and Moloch count even if you leave or your
  connection drops in the middle.
- The world chat box no longer covers the Main yard and Map buttons on short windows.
- A player's name no longer gets squeezed in a mail thread.
- The game loads in older browsers again.
- Attacks keep running while the game's tab is hidden.
- Attacks have enough time to save their result at the end, also under Declare War.
- Attacking a healed tribe camp straight from the map now fights the healed camp, and
  no longer restarts its 12 hours.
- Bug report screenshots now show your yard and windows properly.
