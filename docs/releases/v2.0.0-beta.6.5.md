# v2.0.0-beta.6.5

Map Room 2:
- Land bonuses to resources are smaller: forest, rocks and marsh now give +25% (was +50%), and resource nodes give +50% (was +100%). Building health, tower damage and tower range from land are unchanged.
- Camps and outposts on open ground now say "Open ground · no bonus" in their pop-up, instead of showing no land row.
