# v2.0.0-beta.9

## Move monsters on the map
- Moving monsters between your yards now happens **on Map Room 2 itself**. Tap one of your
  yards, then **Move monsters from here** or **Bring monsters here**. This replaces the old
  Move Monsters window.
- Each of your yards shows a **steel plate** with its biggest monster groups, a room bar
  and its free room. A tray at the bottom has one chip per monster type.
- **Bring to**: every other yard with monsters starts ticked. Tap a yard on the map to
  untick it. Your main yard gives first, then the nearest outposts.
- **Move from**: tap the yard that should receive the monsters. Tap another yard to
  change it.
- Tap a chip to set how many: **−**, **+** and **Max** stop where the next monster would
  not fit. **All** fills the room with every type in turn.
- Moves are **free and instant**. Yards under attack can't send or receive monsters
  until the attack ends.

## Smart moving for your own attacks
- When you attack a Map Room 2 wild camp **by hand** and you have a saved Repeat plan for
  it, the game offers **Bring saved army?**: it moves the plan's monsters into Flinger
  range from your other yards, then starts the attack. **Skip** opens the usual Attack
  window and moves nothing.
- If the army can't be gathered, the Attack window says what is short or where there is
  no room.
- Turn it off in **Settings, Attacks, Smart moving**. It is on by default.

## Hatchery Control Center levels 2 and 3
- The HCC can now be upgraded to **level 2** (8M Twigs, Pebbles and Putty, 2 days, Town
  Hall 6 and four level 3 Hatcheries) and **level 3** (16M each, 4 days, Town Hall 8).
- Queue: level 1 holds **7 stacks of 20**, level 2 **10 stacks of 20**, level 3 **10
  stacks of 30**.
- **Presets**: save a monster mix in the **Presets** tab of the Hatch tab and send it to
  the queue with one tap. Level 1 holds **2 presets**, level 2 **4**, level 3 **6**.
  Send adds the preset to the end of the queue, as far as room and Goo allow.
- At **level 3**, your main yard's HCC **runs your outposts' hatcheries from home**: pick
  an outpost in the Hatch tab. Only outposts with their own HCC can be run this way.
- **Outpost HCCs** go to level 2 (1M each, 2 days, two level 3 Hatcheries). All your
  outposts share one preset list.
- In the Modern look, HCC levels 2 and 3 are new 3D buildings.
- The Inferno's Incubator Control Centre stays at level 1 for now.

## Infernal Lab
- A new building in the Inferno: the **Infernal Lab**, from **Under Hall 5**, one per
  Inferno. Level 1 costs 100,000 Bone and 100,000 Coal; level 2 300,000 each; level 3
  600,000 each (levels 2 and 3 need Under Hall 6).
- It researches **one ability for each of the 8 Inferno monsters**, paid in **Sulfur**, up
  to 3 ranks (Balthazar 2). Ranks work when attacking and defending unless marked
  "attacking only".
  - **Spurtz, Fury**: faster and harder-hitting every second it fights, up to **+5%, +8%
    or +20%**.
  - **Zagnoid, Regeneration**: heals **1%, 1.5% or 2%** of its health every second.
  - **Malphus, Dodge**: **10%, 20% or 30%** of hits miss it. Burns and poison still land.
  - **Valgos, Wall Crusher**: a blow on a wall also hits **1, 2 or 3 more walls** nearby.
    Attacking only.
  - **Balthazar, Monster Hunter**: **2x or 3x damage** to monsters and champions.
  - **Grokus, Shockwave**: **30%, 45% or 60%** of each blow also hits nearby buildings.
    Attacking only.
  - **Sabnox, Burn**: what it hits burns for **10%, 20% or 30%** of the blow every
    second for 4 seconds. Burns don't stack; a new hit restarts it.
  - **King Wormzer, Quake Splash**: its blows splash nearby buildings, **x1, x2 or x4**,
    wider each rank. Attacking only.
- **Two changes for monsters you already have:** without research, **Balthazar now does
  normal damage to monsters** (it did 3x), and **King Wormzer no longer splashes** (it
  did x4). Research brings both back.
- Moloch's raiders keep their strength: their Balthazar hits monsters at 3x and their
  King Wormzer splashes at x4.
- In the Modern look the Infernal Lab is a new 3D building; Classic uses the Flash Monster
  Lab picture.

## Test your yard with friends
- Your **Wild Monster Baiter** can now invite **friends and alliance mates** to test your
  main yard. An invite lasts **24 hours**, with any number of tests.
- They attack with a **free made-up army**, up to the size your Baiter allows. Nothing is
  lost or won on either side.
- The Baiter window has three new tabs: **Invite**, **Invited me** and **Results**. In
  Results you can **watch the replay** and read the report of every test. Tests are kept
  7 days.
- You don't need a Baiter of your own to accept an invite.

## Switching between your yards
- The yard switcher is now one capsule: **< house 1/12 >**. The arrows step through your
  main yard, your pinned outposts, then the rest, and go round at the ends.
- The middle shows where you are and opens your yard list. The yard's name shows for a
  moment when you arrive.

## Tower tiers in Classic
- In the Classic look, every tiered tower now shows a small plate: **I**, **II** or
  **III**, the same tiers the Modern look shows as plain, gold and black.
- Cannon, Sniper, Bunker, Laser, Tesla, Aerial Defense, Railgun, Magma, and the Inferno's
  Blast Tower and Sharpshooter.

## Feeding your champion
- When you feed monsters to your champion, they **walk from their pens to the cage and
  burst into blood**, as in Flash. The feed window stays open.

## Faster loading
- **Signing in is faster**: the first download is about 7 times smaller. The rest of the
  game loads while you type your password. In our slow-phone test, the sign-in screen
  showed in about 2.5 seconds instead of 7.
- **Buildings load first**; monsters, raids, turkeys and music wait until your yard is
  drawn.
- On **Low and Medium** quality, Modern buildings use smaller pictures.
- Pictures, fonts and sounds stay in your browser's cache for longer.
- Fewer requests when your yard opens and when you come home.

## Improvements
- Fewer **black or missing towers** on phones: the big tower pictures are split for phones
  with smaller graphics limits.
- Computers **without a graphics card** start on **Low** quality.
- **Repeat attack** checks your saved plan against the camp before it starts. If a fling
  would land on a building, it asks you to attack once by hand on a fresh camp. Nothing is
  moved or charged.
- **Krallen in the Descent**: her loot aura now counts the same on your screen as on the
  server, so Descent attacks with Krallen are no longer flagged by mistake.
- **Splash on buildings** (King Wormzer, Grokus's Shockwave and other splashing monsters)
  now reaches the same buildings as in Flash.
- An **Eye-ra**'s blast reaches the defender it fights from **30**, as in Flash (was 50).
- Collecting on an Inferno yard throws **Inferno resource balls** (Bone, Coal, Sulfur,
  Magma), as in Flash.
- **Alliance goals** are harder: Medium goals need 5 times as much and Hard goals 10
  times as much. Easy goals and the points paid stay the same, and goals already set keep
  their targets. The goal picker shows only **Easy**, **Medium** and **Hard**.
- In the Modern look, the **Inferno General Store** has a new look.
- The "Your yard was attacked" banner no longer covers open windows.
- The **Build window**, the phone building **placement bar**, the **More** menu, the
  **Monsters** tabs and the **Lab** buttons fit small and short screens.
- In **Classic**, champion and Housing text is easier to read.
- "Could not reach the server" messages no longer show a web address.
- Before planned maintenance, a **countdown** shows on every screen, and you are signed
  out when it ends.
- **Chat**: some links now show as [link removed].
- Replays of defences from before this update can no longer be watched.
