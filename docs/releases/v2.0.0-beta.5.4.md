# v2.0.0-beta.5.4

Fixes (players reported them):
- Yard Planner on a phone: moving a building no longer opens its info window over the yard, so you
  can line it up and place it anywhere. Tap the yard after placing it to bring the window back.
- Fomor on foot (levels 1 and 2) no longer gets stuck beside a wall without attacking while it
  follows a wounded monster. It now breaks the wall in its way and carries on. Older saved defence
  replays can no longer be watched, because the battle rules changed.
- Leaving a battle never shows it undone: your yard waits for the battle to be saved before it loads.
- World chat on short screens: the open chat box stays above the Main yard and Map buttons.
