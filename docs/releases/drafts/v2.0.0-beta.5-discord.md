## Main announcement

```text
@everyone **Beta 5 is live: the Inferno is back!**

**The Inferno**
• At Town Hall 5 a cave opens on your yard. After Moloch's five raids (win or lose), go down into the **Descent**: 13 of Moloch's yards in a row.
• Beat the last one to capture your own **Inferno yard**: Bone, Coal, Sulfur and Magma, the Under Hall, and eight Inferno monsters up to King Wormzer.
• Attack Moloch's tribes and other players' Inferno yards. Send Inferno monsters up to your main yard.
• **Korath**, the fourth champion, unlocks once you own an Inferno yard.
• On your main yard: Quake and Magma Towers, plus **siege weapons**: Marilyn Monstroe, the Loot-O-Tron 5000 and the Candy Jars.
• Flash rules, prices and times. Changes: the Descent burn from yard 7 is real, you can buy extra Inferno workers with Shiny, and a few Inferno quests give Shiny.

**Also new**
• **Music is back**, with a new yard tune and Flash's collect sounds. Switch them off in the Account menu.
• **Modern or Classic** in Account Settings: Classic brings back the original Flash look and yard tune.
• **Quality** setting (High / Medium / Low) for slow internet.
• New tower shots, Town Hall pictures for levels 1-10, and repainted champions.
• Fewer words, more pictures. One-click Champion Cage and Chamber windows, one tab per hatchery.
• Fixed: big painted monsters showing as a black box on some phones.
```

```text
**Inferno tips**

• In the Descent you need 90% damage to move on. A yard you don't beat stays damaged for your next try.
• From Descent yard 7 your surface monsters burn. Inferno monsters and Korath don't.
• **3D towers** are being built: Cannon, Sniper, Laser, Aerial Defense, Railgun, the Spurtz Cannons and the Inferno towers. They're not switched on yet; we plan to bring them in beta 6 or 7.
```
