**Title:** Beta 5 is out: the Inferno is back

Hi everyone,

Beta 5 is live. Here's what's in it.

**The Inferno is back.** At Town Hall 5 a cave opens on your yard and Moloch's monsters start
coming out. After five raids (win or lose, each one counts), go down into the Descent: 13 of Moloch's yards, one after
another. Beat the last one and you capture your own Inferno yard on the lava, with Bone, Coal,
Sulfur and Magma, the Under Hall, and eight Inferno monsters up to King Wormzer. The rules, prices
and times follow the original game, with a few changes:

- From the 7th Descent yard your surface monsters really burn (Flash only showed a warning).
- You can buy up to four extra Inferno workers with Shiny.
- A few of the biggest Inferno quests give some Shiny too.

Owning an Inferno yard also unlocks **Korath**, the fourth champion, repainted at every level. Your
main yard gets the Quake Tower, the Magma Tower and the **siege weapons**: Marilyn Monstroe, the
Loot-O-Tron 5000 and the Candy Jars. You can also attack Moloch's tribes and other players'
Inferno yards.

**Music is back.** The yard, attack and raid music plays again, with a new yard tune and Flash's
collect sounds. You can switch music and sounds off in the Account menu.

**Modern or Classic.** One choice in Account Settings. Modern shows the new art and effects.
Classic brings back the original Flash look and the old yard tune.

**Also new:**
- A Quality setting (High, Medium, Low) for slow internet.
- New painted shots for six of the towers.
- New Town Hall pictures for levels 1 to 10.
- Gorgo, Drull, Fomor and Krallen repainted at more levels.
- Fewer words and more pictures across the game.
- One-click Champion Cage and Champion Chamber windows, and one tab per hatchery.
- Raising a champion gives it to you straight away again, like the original game. No more egg.
- A Clear all button for hatchery queues, with your goo given back.
- Friends: help a friend and send gifts after 12 hours of friendship, with no account-age wait.
- Painted window frames: steel in Modern, parchment in Classic.
- Big painted monsters no longer show as a black box on some phones and older computers.

**3D towers (Modern).** The Cannon, Sniper, Laser, Aerial Defense, Railgun, Spurtz, Black Spurtz,
Tesla and Bunker are now 3D models with turrets that turn to aim, and shots leave from the barrel.
The Tesla's coil film rolls while it fires, and the Bunker's shutters roll up to let its defenders
out. The Inferno towers grow taller with each level, and the Magma Tower has a new volcanic-rock
look. Classic keeps the original Flash towers.

**Also in this beta:**
- A Yard Planner for your Inferno yard (Under Hall 3, one per yard).
- Yards load faster: buildings show first, monsters follow.
- Bunker defenders now wait until the door is fully open before they come out, as in Flash.
- Map Room 2: a construction sign shows on outposts being upgraded.

The full list of changes is in the patch notes. If something breaks, please use "Report a bug" in
the Account menu, or tell us on Discord.

Thank you for supporting the project. See you in the Inferno.
