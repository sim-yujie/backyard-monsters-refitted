**Title:** Backyard Monsters Revamped beta 5: The Inferno is here!

Hi everyone!

Beta 5 is out now at play.bymrevamped.com, and it brings back the biggest piece of the original
game that was still missing: the Inferno. Fight down the Descent, capture your own yard on the
lava, raise Korath and fire siege weapons. Music is back too, you can switch the whole game to the
original Flash look, and most towers are now 3D. Here's a quick look.

**The Inferno is back**

At Town Hall 5 a cave opens on your yard and Moloch's monsters start coming out. They raid you five
times, and win or lose, every raid counts. After that, the cave is yours to enter.

[Picture: card 01, Inferno]
_Caption: The cave opens at Town Hall 5._

**The Descent**

The Descent is 13 of Moloch's yards, one after another. Do 90% damage to open the next one. From the
7th yard your surface monsters really burn and slowly lose health (Flash only showed a warning).

[Picture: card 02, Descent]
_Caption: 13 yards down. From the 7th, your monsters burn._

**Your own Inferno yard**

Beat the last Descent yard and you capture your own yard on the lava, with Bone, Coal, Sulfur and
Magma, the Under Hall, and eight Inferno monsters up to King Wormzer. You can buy up to four extra
Inferno workers with Shiny, attack Moloch's tribes, and attack other players' Inferno yards. A Yard
Planner comes with Under Hall 3.

[Picture: card 03, Inferno yard]
_Caption: Your Inferno yard, with its own resources and buildings._

**Korath, the fourth champion**

Owning an Inferno yard unlocks Korath. Raise him in your Champion Cage. He is repainted at every
level, stomp included.

[Picture: card 04, Korath]
_Caption: Korath joins Gorgo, Drull and Fomor._

**Siege weapons**

Your main yard gets the Quake Tower, the Magma Tower, the Siege Works and the Siege Factory. Fire a
siege weapon from the Siege button in an attack:

- Marilyn Monstroe draws defending monsters to her, then blows up.
- The Loot-O-Tron 5000 latches on to the Town Hall and pulls out extra loot.
- Candy Jars trap defensive towers until they shoot their way out.

[Picture: card 05, Siege weapons]
_Caption: Marilyn Monstroe, the Loot-O-Tron 5000 and the Candy Jars._

**Music is back**

The yard, attack and raid music plays again, with a new yard tune and Flash's collect sounds. The
Inferno has its own music. Switch music and sounds off in the Account menu or with the speaker
button by the zoom buttons.

[Picture: card 06, Music]
_Caption: Music and sounds are back._

**Modern or Classic**

One choice in Account Settings. Modern shows the new art and effects. Classic brings back the
original Flash look wherever Flash had one: buildings, monsters, champions, battle effects and the
old yard tune.

[Picture: card 07, Modern or Classic]
_Caption: The Town Hall in Classic (left) and Modern (right)._

**3D towers**

In Modern, the Cannon, Sniper Tower, Laser, Aerial Defense, Railgun, Spurtz, Black Spurtz, Tesla,
Bunker and the Inferno towers are now 3D, with turrets that turn to aim and shots that leave from the
barrel. The Tesla's coil film rolls while it fires, and the Bunker's shutters roll up to let its
defenders out. The Inferno towers grow taller with each level. Classic keeps the original Flash
towers.

[Picture: card 08, 3D towers]
_Caption: The new 3D towers in Modern._

**Quality setting and faster loading**

Choose High, Medium or Low in Account Settings. Medium and Low load smaller pictures and lighter
music, for slow internet. Yards also load faster: buildings show first and monsters follow.

[Picture: card 09, Quality]
_Caption: High, Medium or Low, your choice._

**Fewer words, more pictures**

Amounts, costs and stats now show as painted icons with numbers on the top bar, in building windows,
in the Shop and on the attack screen.

[Picture: card 10, Fewer words]
_Caption: Icons and numbers instead of long lines of text._

**Also in this update**

New painted shots for six towers, new Town Hall pictures for levels 1 to 10, Gorgo, Drull, Fomor and
Krallen repainted at more levels, painted window frames (steel in Modern, parchment in Classic),
status stickers over a monster's health bar, one-click Champion Cage and Champion Chamber windows,
one tab per hatchery, a Clear all button for hatchery queues with your goo given back, champions
that you get straight away again when raised, like the original game, friend help and gifts after
12 hours instead of a day, Bunker defenders that wait for the door to open fully, a construction
sign on Map Room 2 outposts being upgraded, and a fix for big painted monsters showing as a black
box on some phones and older computers. The full list is in the patch notes.

**This is still a beta**

Things will break. If you find a bug, please press "Report a bug" in the Account menu, or email
simyujie@gmail.com.
