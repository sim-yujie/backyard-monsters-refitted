**Title:** Backyard Monsters Revamped beta 6: a brand-new Map Room and a yard in 3D

Hi everyone!

Beta 6 is out now at play.bymrevamped.com. Map Room 2 has a whole new painted look, most
of your yard is now in 3D, and there's lots more to do: daily quests, new Monster Lab
abilities, and the original decorations back in the shop. The old attack sounds are
back too. Here's a quick look.

**A new Map Room 2**

The map is now one painted landscape, with hills, cliffs, forests, beaches and rivers,
and you look across it at a tilt. Forests, rocks and marsh change a cell's defence and
income, and cells next to your own land defend better. Prefer it flat? Switch it off
for your account.

[Picture: 01, Map Room 2]
_Caption: The new Map Room 2, in Modern._

**Your yard in 3D**

In Modern, your Twig Snapper, Pebble Shiner, Putty Squisher, Goo Factory, Silo,
Hatchery, Academy, Champion Cage, Monster Lab and many more are now 3D, with moving
parts. Every building has its own damaged and destroyed look. Classic still has the
original Flash buildings.

[Picture: 02, 3D buildings]
_Caption: A yard in 3D, in Modern._

**Quests**

A 7-day login card, 5 daily quests and 3 weekly quests, all paying Shiny. Don't like a
daily quest? Swap it once a day for free.

[Picture: 03, Quests]
_Caption: Daily and weekly quests pay Shiny._

**New Monster Lab abilities**

Four new upgrades: swarming Pokeys attack faster, an Octo-ooze weakens whatever kills
it, Ichi hits back with Thorns, and Crabatron can land double-damage critical hits.

[Picture: 04, Monster Lab]
_Caption: Swarm, Weakening Ooze, Thorns and Critical Hits._

**Decorations are back**

The original game's decorations are on sale again for Shiny, in the Build menu's
Decorations tab.

[Picture: 05, Decorations]
_Caption: Dress up your yard._

**The old sounds are back**

Tower shots, monster deaths, bombs and Wormzer sound like the original again, and loot
flies out of buildings as you attack. Music and sound effects each have their own volume
slider.

[Picture: 06, Loot and sound]
_Caption: Loot pops out of every building you hit._

**Also in this update**

- **Wild tribes:** every tribe camp has a new hand-made yard at each level, and the tribes are rebalanced.
- **The Juicer show:** monsters walk in, get blended, and goo and giblets spurt out.
- **Font:** pick Game font, Clear or Extra readable in Account settings.
- **Tower range:** hover over one of your defences to see how far it reaches.
- **One "More" button** for Quests, Layout, Goals, alerts, Defences and Mail, and a tidier bottom row on phones.
- **World chat** can be switched off in Account settings.
- **Fixes:** attacks on wild tribes, the Descent and Moloch count even if your connection drops, and the game loads in older browsers again.

The full list is in the patch notes.

**This is still a beta**

Things will break. If you find a bug, please press "Report a bug" in the Account menu, or
email simyujie@gmail.com.
