## Main announcement

Picture: `01-map-room-2.jpg`.

```text
@everyone **Beta 6 is live: a new Map Room 2 and your yard in 3D!**

**New Map Room 2** (Modern)
• One painted landscape: hills, cliffs, forests, beaches and rivers, seen at a tilt. Prefer it flat? Switch it off for your account.
• Forest, rock and marsh cells change defence and income. Cells next to your own land defend better.

**Your yard in 3D** (Modern)
• Twig Snapper, Pebble Shiner, Putty Squisher, Goo Factory, Silo, Hatchery, Academy, Champion Cage, Monster Lab and more, with moving parts and their own damaged looks. Classic keeps the Flash buildings.

**New things to do**
• **Quests**: a 7-day login card, 5 daily and 3 weekly quests, all paying Shiny.
• **Monster Lab**: Pokey Swarm, Octo-ooze Weakening Ooze, Ichi Thorns and Crabatron Critical Hits.
• **Decorations** are back in the shop, for Shiny.
• Every wild tribe camp has a new yard, and the tribes are rebalanced.

**Also new**
• The original attack sounds are back, and loot pops out of buildings as you attack.
• Separate volume sliders for music and sound effects.
• Font choice: Game font, Clear or Extra readable.
• Hover over a defence to see its range.
• One "More" button for Quests, Layout, Goals, Defences and Mail.
• World chat can be switched off in Account settings.
• Fixed: attacks count even if your connection drops; the game loads in older browsers again.

Full patch notes: <PATREON LINK>
Found a bug? Use "Report a bug" in the Account menu.
```
