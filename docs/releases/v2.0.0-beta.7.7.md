# v2.0.0-beta.7.7

Fixes:
- Repeat attack: only yards whose own Flinger reaches the target send monsters, the same yards the Attack window counts. If those yards do not have enough monsters, Repeat attack shows how many you are short and does not attack.
- Attacking with a Drull: when the defender's Fomor walks out and shoots your Drull, the Drull now hits back at once from where it stands, as in Flash. Before, it walked up to the Fomor first.
- Map on a phone held upright: the top bar is one band and takes less of the screen. Twigs and Pebbles sit over Putty and Goo on the left, Map/Yard in the middle, your picture over Shiny on the right.
- Map: Glimmers are no longer shown on the map bar. They buy decorations, which are placed in your yard.
- Map on an iPad (upright or in Split View) and on a small phone on its side: Shiny is no longer cut off at the right of the top bar.
- Map on a phone: the exact-amount bubble and the Account menu open under the top bar instead of over it.
