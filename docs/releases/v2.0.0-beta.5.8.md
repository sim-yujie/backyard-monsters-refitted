# v2.0.0-beta.5.8

New players:
- Skipping the guide during its practice attack no longer ends that attack with "This yard has changed
  since the attack began". The attack now finishes normally.

Chat:
- The word filter now also catches disguised swear words, such as letters swapped for numbers or
  symbols, or spaced out.
- Some ordinary words are no longer starred out, such as puto, pula, suka and pawn.
- Game team tools: swearing and slurs in World and Philippines chat now reach the game team, who can
  warn, pause or ban. Chat is kept for 90 days for this.
