# v2.0.0-beta.6.13

Fixes:
- Healing your champion in battle now sticks. When a Vorg or Zafreeti heals your champion during an attack, the champion keeps that health afterwards, as it did in the original game. Before this fix, the healing was lost when the attack ended and only the damage was kept. This applies to Map Room 1, Map Room 2 and the Descent.
